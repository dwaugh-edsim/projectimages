🤝 The Political War Room

Your team must complete all three deliverables below before you can face the press. Assign one role per person. If you have 2 people, one person takes two roles.

⚠️ Warning: Once you lock in, a live journalist will challenge you on everything you wrote. Be specific, be real, and be ready to defend it.

📣

Director of Communications

Write the opening statement a journalist will read on live TV to introduce your party. This is your first impression — make it count.

💡 Start with: "The people of Nova Scotia deserve a government that..."

🛡️

Strategy & Policy Lead

List exactly three promises your party is making to voters. These are non-negotiable — the journalist will ask about each one.

💡 Be specific! "Fix healthcare" is weak. "Build 3 new walk-in clinics in HRM by 2028" is strong.

💰

Financial Chief

The journalist will ask: "How are you paying for this?" You need a real answer. Vague = crisis.

💡 Options: raise income tax, cut military spending, apply for federal grants, carbon tax revenue, new sales tax on luxury goods...
