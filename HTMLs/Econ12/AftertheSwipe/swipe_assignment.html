Consumer Credit History

1950
Diners Club launches the first multi-merchant charge card, transforming consumer transaction habits.
1978
The Supreme Court Marquette decision allows national banks to export interest rates across state lines, leading to higher credit card APRs.
1989
FICO score system is introduced, establishing a standardized risk assessment algorithm for lenders.
2009
The federal CARD Act is signed, limiting rate hikes on existing balances and banning deceptive advertising on campus.

Key Terms Glossary

APR (Annual Percentage Rate)
The yearly interest rate charged on borrowing. For credit cards, this is usually calculated daily if you carry a balance from month to month.
Credit Utilization Rate
The percentage of your total available credit limit currently being used. Keeping utilization below 30% is a key factor in maintaining a high credit score.
Minimum Payment
The smallest amount you must pay by the due date to keep your account in good standing. Paying only this amount maximizes the interest you owe over time.
Credit Score Gauge
A mathematical representation of creditworthiness. Key scoring components include payment history (35%) and utilization (30%).
