The 5-Page Strategy

  1. Home: The Mission & Bios
  2. Investigation: Human Impact & UN Goals
  3. Canada's Response: Evaluating Policies
  4. Action Project: Your Video or PDF Briefing
  5. Sources: Give credit where it's due

Portfolio Requirements Checklist

  • 1. Home (The Mission): The Hook, Mission Statement, and Team Bios.
  • 2. The Investigation Log: Global Connection, Human Impact (Lived Experience), and Consequences of Inaction.
  • 3. Canada's Response: Evaluation of a current law or program backed by Evidence.
  • 4. The Action Project: Embed a 60-second PSA video OR a 1-page MLA Briefing PDF. Also include a Map of the issue.
  • 5. Final Reflection & Sources: Ensure all sources used are cited.
