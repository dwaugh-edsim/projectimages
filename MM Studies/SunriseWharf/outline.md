## ⚓ THE WHARF ACCORD: 6 Chapters

### Chapter 1: The Claim
* **Primary:** Sunrise Collective cites 1760 Treaty "Truckhouse" clause + *Marshall I*: "We have a right to land and trade our catch here."
* **Legal:** *Marshall I* (1999): Treaty rights to a "moderate livelihood" include access to infrastructure necessary to exercise that right?
* **Intel:** Community survey: 68% of locals say "everyone should follow the same rules." 32% say "Treaty rights come first."
* **Judgment Box:** *Does a Treaty right to trade imply a right to use public wharf infrastructure?* [Yes / No / Needs Consultation]

### Chapter 2: The Rules
* **Primary:** Clearwater Harbor Bylaw §4.2: "All commercial landings must occur within DFO seasonal windows."
* **Legal:** *Sparrow Test* framework: Can a local rule infringe a Treaty right if it serves a "valid objective" (safety/order) and is "minimally impairing"?
* **Intel:** Wharf log: 3 near-miss incidents last season during off-season landings. Safety is a genuine concern.
* **Judgment Box:** *Can a local safety rule limit a Treaty right, and under what conditions?* [Yes, if… / No, because… / Defer to Crown]

### Chapter 3: The Flashpoint
* **Primary:** Archival photo/video prompt: A recent standoff at the wharf gate. Tensions escalated; no injuries, but trust eroded.
* **Legal:** *Duty to Consult & Accommodate*: When Crown knowledge of a potential right exists, authorities must engage before acting.
* **Intel:** Social media scrape: #WharfStandoff trending locally. Both sides feel unheard. De-escalation is urgent.
* **Judgment Box:** *What is the Committee's first step to de-escalate while upholding the law?* [Pause all landings / Create temporary access lane / Convene emergency consultation]

### Chapter 4: The Duty
* **Primary:** Mi'kmaw Studies 11 resource: "Brother to Brother" treaty relationship + Wampum symbolism of ongoing partnership.
* **Legal:** *Haida Nation* (2004): Honour of the Crown requires meaningful consultation, even at municipal levels implementing policy.
* **Intel:** Stakeholder map: Sunrise Collective, Seaview Union, DFO, Municipality, Elders Council—all have different expectations of "consultation."
* **Judgment Box:** *What does "meaningful consultation" look like for this wharf?* [One-time meeting / Ongoing joint committee / Elder-led process]

### Chapter 5: The Precedent
* **Primary:** Side-by-side: 1760 Treaty text (oral/wampum record) vs. *Marshall I* SCC summary.
* **Legal:** "Empty Box vs. Full Box": Were Treaty rights dormant until courts defined them, or were they always inherent and sovereign?
* **Intel:** Student poll (pre-sim): 45% think "Treaty rights are historical." 55% think "Treaty rights are living." Views shift after evidence.
* **Judgment Box:** *Is the 1760 Treaty a "living agreement" that applies to modern wharf access?* [Yes / No / It depends on…]

### Chapter 6: The Path Forward
* **Primary:** Draft protocol templates from other coastal communities (successes and failures).
* **Legal:** UNDRIP Art. 21 + *Marshall II*: Economic reconciliation requires accommodation, not assimilation.
* **Intel:** Risk assessment: If the Committee does nothing, the standoff escalates. If they act unilaterally, they risk legal challenge.
* **Judgment Box:** *Draft your 3-sentence Wharf Protocol.* Must: (1) Acknowledge Treaty right, (2) Address safety/order, (3) Include one concrete consultation step.
