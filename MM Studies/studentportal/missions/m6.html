TOP SECRET // MISSION 06

The Secret Path

"The Secret Path" tells the story of Chanie Wenjack, a boy who died while trying to walk home from a residential school in Ontario. His story helped many people understand the pain of the residential school system.

Your mission is to map out Chanie’s journey. You do not need to read the entire graphic novel for this mission. Instead, focus on the animated film or the key lyrics from the project.

📺 Watch 'The Secret Path' Animated Film (1 hour)

📖 Chanie's Journey: Key Facts

The School: Cecilia Jeffrey Residential School in Kenora, Ontario.
The Path: Chanie followed the railroad tracks, trying to reach his home 600km away.
The 7 Matches: He only had seven matches in a jar to keep himself warm.
The Legacy: His story led to the first-ever inquiry into the treatment of children in schools.

Source: SecretPath.ca
