TOP SECRET // MISSION 04

Dream School Design

In the past, schools weren't always safe or welcoming for Mi'kmaw children. Today, we have the chance to design spaces that celebrate culture and make everyone feel proud.

Your mission is to design a Dream School that celebrates Mi'kmaw culture and makes every student feel safe and excited to learn.

💡 Dream School Feature Bank

The Circle Room: A room for talking circles and community meetings.
The Medicine Garden: A space to grow sage, cedar, and tobacco.
Mwikma'ki Map: A large floor mural of traditional territories.
Smudging Station: A safe place for traditional smudging ceremonies.
Step 1: The Environment
What colors and natural materials (like wood or stone) would you use to make the building feel connected to the land?
Step 2: The Symbols
What Mi'kmaw symbols or art would you put in the hallways? (e.g., the 8-pointed star, petroglyphs, or community flags).
Step 3: The Feeling
How would a student know they are in a Mi'kmaw school the moment they walk through the door?
