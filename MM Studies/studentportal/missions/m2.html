Modern Petroglyphs

For thousands of years, Mi'kmaw people carved stories into stone (petroglyphs) at sites like Kejimkujik. These symbols recorded everything from hunters to spiritual beings.

Your mission is to design 3 original Modern Petroglyphs. What symbols would represent Mi'kmaw life in 2026?

💡 Modern Object Bank

Laptop: Symbol of modern knowledge and connection.
Wind Turbine: Symbol of sustainable energy and the land.
Hockey Stick: Symbol of community gathering and sport.
School Bus: Symbol of modern education and the journey.

Step 2: Community & Culture
