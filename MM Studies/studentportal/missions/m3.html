TOP SECRET // MISSION 03

The Activist Trading Card

History is made by people who stand up for their community. In this mission, you will design a "Trading Card" for a Mi'kmaw leader or activist who shows the Seven Sacred Teachings in action.

Pick Your Activist:

Donald Marshall Jr.

Wrongfully convicted, he fought for Mi'kmaw fishing rights and won a landmark Supreme Court case.

Nora Bernard

A Shubenacadie survivor who led the largest class-action lawsuit in Canadian history for Residential School survivors.

Rita Joe

Known as the "Gentle Warrior," her poetry helped reclaim the Mi'kmaw language and identity.

Daniel N. Paul

A powerful historian and author of "We Were Not the Savages" who changed how Mi'kmaw history is told.

💡 Virtue & Strength Bank

🐢 Truth: Speaking up for what is right even when it's hard.
🐻 Courage: Facing fears to protect the community or the land.
🦅 Love: Acting with kindness and care for all living things.
👤 Honesty: Being true to yourself and your promises.
Step 1: Choose Your Leader
Pick one person from the list above or someone you know who works for the community.
Step 2: Assign a Virtue
Which Teaching defines them? (Truth, Courage, Love, Wisdom, Humility, Respect, or Honesty)
Step 3: Design the "Stats"
What are their 3 "Power Strengths"? (e.g., Justice +100, Courage +99)
