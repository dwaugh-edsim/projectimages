Myth vs. Truth

History is often told through "Myths" (stories that are popular but not always true) and "Truths" (stories backed by primary sources). The story of Pocahontas is a classic example of a Disney myth.

Your mission is to compare the "Myth" of Pocahontas with the "Truth" of Indigenous history.

🍎 Pocahontas: The Real History

The Age: She was only 10 or 11 when she met John Smith, not a grown woman.
The Capture: She was kidnapped by the English in 1613 and held for ransom.
The Name: Her real name was Matoaka. Pocahontas was a nickname meaning "playful one".
The Death: She died in England at age 21, likely from a disease she caught there.
