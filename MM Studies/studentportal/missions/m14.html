The Ash Basket Weaver

Baskets were woven from thin strips of black ash wood. The "checkerboard" pattern makes them strong and beautiful.

Your mission is to weave a basket. Click a row to lay a horizontal strip, or click a column to weave vertically. Alternate Over and Under to create the pattern.
