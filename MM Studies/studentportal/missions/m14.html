The Ash Basket Weaver

Baskets were woven from thin strips of black ash wood. The "checkerboard" pattern makes them strong and beautiful.

Your mission is to weave a basket using the "Pattern Coder." Click the grid to toggle between Over (White) and Under (Purple).

Pattern Coder

Click cells to weave your pattern

Step 1: Start
Click the "White" strip to begin your weave.
Step 2: Weave
Click the "Purple" strip to go over it.
Step 3: Check
Make sure the pattern goes Over-Under-Over-Under.
