Ipperwash Ticker

The Ipperwash Crisis was a land dispute in Ontario where the Stoney Point Ojibway community protested the theft of their land during WWII. It led to a major inquiry and a call for better land-claim systems.

Your mission is to create a series of News Headlines (a "Ticker") that explains the crisis from the perspective of the land protectors.

📑 Ipperwash Crisis: Key Facts

The Stolen Land: In 1942, the army took the Stoney Point reserve to build a base, promising to return it. They didn't.
Dudley George: An unarmed Ojibway land protector who was shot and killed by police during the protest.
The Inquiry: A 2007 report proved the government and police acted unfairly and ignored land rights.
Return of Land: In 2016, the government finally signed a deal to return the land and pay for damages.
Step 1: The Protest Begins
Write a headline for when the community first re-entered the park to reclaim their land.
Step 2: The Inquiry
Write a headline for when the truth finally came out about how the land was stolen and the events of the protest.
Step 3: The Future
Write a headline for the day the land is finally returned to its rightful owners.
