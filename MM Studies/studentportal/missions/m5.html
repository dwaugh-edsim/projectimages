Storyboard of Life

The Mi'kmaw Creation Story is told in seven levels (Sun, Earth, Glooscap, Grandmother, Young Man, Mother, and the Community). Each level teaches us about our relationship to the world.

Your mission is to create a 6-square Storyboard of the Creation Story. Use only symbols—no words allowed!

🌟 The 7 Levels of Creation

1. Sun: The giver of light and life.
2. Earth: Our Mother who sustains us.
3. Glooscap: The first human, created by lightning.
4. Grandmother: Symbol of wisdom and the past.
5. Young Man: Symbol of action and the future.
6. Mother: Symbol of love and the present.
7. Community: All of us together.
Step 1: The First Symbols
Level 1 (The Sun/Giver of Life) and Level 2 (The Earth/Mother). How would you draw them as simple symbols?
Step 2: The Arrival
Level 3 (Glooscap). He was formed by a bolt of lightning hitting the sand. How would you show this transformation?
Step 3: The Family
Level 4, 5, and 6 (Grandmother/Wisdom, Young Man/Action, Mother/Love). Pick 3 symbols to represent these essential family members.
