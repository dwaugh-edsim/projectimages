TRC Report Card

The Truth and Reconciliation Commission (TRC) created 94 "Calls to Action" to fix the relationship between Indigenous people and the rest of Canada.

Your mission is to look at the Calls to Action and pick the **3 most important ones** for your community. Give Canada a grade!

📜 TRC Call to Action Categories

Education: Fixing the funding gap for schools on reserves (Calls 6-12).
Language: Protecting and reviving Indigenous languages (Calls 13-17).
Justice: Reducing the number of Indigenous people in prison (Calls 30-42).
Health: Closing the gap in health outcomes (Calls 18-24).

Source: Full TRC PDF

Step 1: Pick the Priority
Which Call to Action do you think would help Mi'kmaw students the most? (e.g., Education, Language, or Health).
Step 2: Give a Grade
On a scale of A to F, how well do you think Canada is doing at fulfilling that Call to Action? Why?
Step 3: The Recommendation
What is one thing the government should do *tomorrow* to make things better?
