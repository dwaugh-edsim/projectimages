Medal of Friendship

When treaties were signed, "Treaty Medals" were often given to Mi'kmaw leaders. They featured symbols of the Sun and the King, representing an agreement that would last as long as the sun shines.

Your mission is to design a **Modern Medal of Friendship**. What symbols would represent an agreement made today?

🤝 Treaty Promise Bank

"To live in peace as long as the sun shines."
"To share the bounty of the land and the waters."
"To listen to each other with respect and honesty."
"To protect the future for the next seven generations."
Step 1: The Core Symbols
Pick two symbols to put on the front of the medal. (e.g., a handshake, the 8-pointed star, or two rivers meeting).
Step 2: The Material
What would this medal be made of today? (e.g., Silver, recycled copper, or even carved wood).
Step 3: The Promise
Write the short sentence that is engraved on the back of the medal. What is the promise we are making to each other?
