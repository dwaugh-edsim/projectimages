The Justice Circle

Restorative Justice (or Sentencing Circles) is a traditional Indigenous way of handling conflict. Instead of focusing on punishment, the circle focuses on healing the harm done to the victim and the community.

Your mission is to map out a Sentencing Circle for a small conflict (like someone breaking a window). Who sits in the circle?

⭕ The Circle Participants

The Elder: Provides wisdom and reminds the circle of the Sacred Teachings.
The Harmed: Shares how they felt and what they need to heal.
The Harmer: Takes responsibility and listens to the impact of their actions.
Community Supporter: Helps ensure the healing plan is followed.
Step 1: The Participants
Name 4 people who must be in the circle to make it fair. (e.g., an Elder, the person who caused the harm, the victim, etc.).
Step 2: The Talking Piece
In a circle, only the person holding the "Talking Piece" (like an Eagle Feather or a stone) can speak. Why is this rule important for healing?
Step 3: The Resolution
What is one "Healing Action" the person could take to fix the harm, instead of just going to jail?
