2050 Vision

Indigenous people have been here for over 10,000 years. As we look towards the next 100 years, what does a thriving, modern Mi'kmaw community look like in the year 2050?

Your mission is to describe or draw the future. How has technology, environment, and culture blended together?

Governance: Digital voting circles for community decisions.
Transport: Solar canoes or electric transit between reserves.
