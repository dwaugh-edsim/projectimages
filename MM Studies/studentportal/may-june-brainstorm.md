# Curriculum-Aligned Brainstorm: May–June (Draeden)
**Source:** Mi’kmaw Studies 11 Guide (2016)  
**Goal:** Replace/Augment the generic portal items with high-authenticity tasks.

---

## 🏛️ Unit: Governance & History (Guide Outcome G5)
*Instead of just a "Community Map," let's use the Guide's "Resilience" focus.*

1.  **The Wampum Message (Visual)**: (Inspired by Guide p. 34) Create a "digital wampum belt" using a grid or pixel art. The belt should tell a story of "Peace" without using any words.
2.  **Annotated Timeline of Change**: (Inspired by Guide p. 38) Draw a 3-step timeline showing how life changed for a Mi'kmaw family because of the Indian Act. Use icons for "Before," "During," and "Now."
3.  **Book of Honour**: (Inspired by Guide p. 38) Create a "Trading Card" for a Mi'kmaw activist or leader. Draw their face on the front and 3 "Power Strengths" on the back.

## 🎨 Unit: Culture & Identity (Guide Outcome C2, C4)
*The guide emphasizes petroglyphs and oral tradition.*

4.  **Modern Petroglyphs**: (Inspired by Guide p. 44) Design 5 original "Modern Petroglyphs." What symbols would represent Mi'kmaw life in 2026? (e.g., a laptop with a Mi'kmaw flag, a modern canoe).
5.  **Elder Profile**: (Inspired by Guide p. 45) Create a visual poster of an Elder (can be someone he knows or a historical figure). Focus on the "Virtues" they show.
6.  **Artistic Evolution**: (Inspired by Guide p. 46) Draw a picture of a traditional Mi'kmaw basket next to a modern piece of Mi'kmaw art. Circle 2 things that are the same in both.

## 🏫 Unit: Education & TRC (Guide Outcome E1, E2)
*Moving from just "gluing" to "meaningful response."*

7.  **Residential School "Dialogue"**: (Inspired by Guide p. 52) Look at a photo of a residential school classroom. Draw two speech bubbles for the students—what are they thinking but not allowed to say?
8.  **Reclaiming the School**: (Inspired by Guide p. 53) Design a "Dream School" for a Mi'kmaw community. What symbols and colors would be on the walls to make it feel safe?

## ✨ Unit: Spirituality (Guide Outcome S1, S2)
*The most visual unit in the curriculum.*

9.  **Creation Story Storyboard**: (Inspired by Guide p. 57) Use 6 squares to draw the Mi'kmaw Creation Story. Use only symbols (Sun, Earth, Glooscap, etc.)—no words allowed.
10. **The Seven Virtues Icons**: (Inspired by Guide p. 57) We already have the animals! Draeden's task: Pick one virtue and draw a "Hero" who shows that virtue in real life.
11. **Spirituality T-Chart**: (Inspired by Guide p. 58) Create a visual T-Chart comparing a Mi'kmaw symbol (like a Cedar branch) with a Catholic symbol (like a Cross).
