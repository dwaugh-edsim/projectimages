### Chapter 1 — The Box in the Closet

Maya had seen the box before, always on the top shelf in her grandmother’s hallway closet, wrapped in an old blue scarf and pushed behind winter boots. No one touched it. No one mentioned it. When her grandmother finally brought it down, she did not open it right away. She just sat at the kitchen table with both hands resting on the lid, as if something inside was still alive.

Inside were small things: a child’s sweater, a faded photograph, a church card, a school report, and one government form with sharp black typing and a heavy stamp across the front. Maya read the file twice before she understood what she was seeing. The child in the report was her grandmother, but the file did not call her Annie Francis. It called her Anne Peters.

That night Maya realized the box was not a keepsake box. It was evidence.
