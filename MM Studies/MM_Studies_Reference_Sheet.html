REFERENCE: CASE CONTEXT

1. Bagot Commission (1844) Recommended separating children from their parents to remove the "influence of the wigwam." TEXTBOOK: Ch. 17, p. 48
2. Three Principles The system was designed for: 1. Isolation, 2. Assimilation, and 3. Christianization. TEXTBOOK: Ch. 17, p. 48-49
3. 1920 Mandatory Attendance Amendment to the Indian Act making school mandatory. Parents were jailed for resisting. TEXTBOOK: Ch. 17, p. 48
4. The Bryce Report (1907) - DEATH RATES Dr. P.H. Bryce reported that death rates in these schools were as high as 24% to 50%, primarily from tuberculosis and neglect. The government ignored his findings. TEXTBOOK: Ch. 17, p. 48-49
5. The Sixties Scoop (1960s-1980s) Mass removal of children into the foster system, often without parental consent. TEXTBOOK: Ch. 7, p. 21
6. The 2012 Reality Indigenous children make up nearly 50% of the foster care system today (The "Millennial Scoop"). TEXTBOOK: Ch. 27, p. 72-73
7. Chanie "Charlie" Wenjack (1966) Chanie ran away from the Cecilia Jeffrey school to find his home 600km away. He was running from isolation and the fear of the system. TEXTBOOK: Ch. 17, p. 48-49 (Context)
