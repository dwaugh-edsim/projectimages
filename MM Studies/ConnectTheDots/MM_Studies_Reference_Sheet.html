REFERENCE: CASE CONTEXT

1. Bagot Commission (1842-1844) Recommended that manual labor schools be established to separate Indigenous children from their parents. TEXTBOOK: Ch. 17, p. 48
2. Three Principles of Residential Schools The system was designed with three goals: 1. Isolation, 2. Assimilation, and 3. Christianization. TEXTBOOK: Ch. 17, p. 48-49
3. 1920 Mandatory Attendance A specific change to the Indian Act that gave the government the power to force every child (7-15) into school. TEXTBOOK: Ch. 17, p. 48
4. The Sixties Scoop (1960s-1980s) A period where thousands of Indigenous children were taken by social workers and placed into foster care. TEXTBOOK: Ch. 7, p. 21
5. The 2012 Reality As of 2012, nearly 50% of children in foster care in Canada are Indigenous. TEXTBOOK: Ch. 27, p. 72-73
6. Chanie "Charlie" Wenjack (1966) Chanie ran away from school because he was desperately lonely and missed his family. He walked for over 36 hours. TEXTBOOK: Ch. 17, p. 48-49 (Context)
