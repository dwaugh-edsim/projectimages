## Unit 10: Residential Schools

**Folder:** `10.  Residential Schools-20250611T175156Z-1-001`

### Overview
This unit explores the history, impact, and legacy of the Canadian residential school system. It covers the educational policies designed to assimilate Indigenous children, the lived experiences of survivors, and the ongoing process of truth and reconciliation.

### Highlights
- **"We Were Children" Movie** — A powerful documentary featuring survivor testimonies
- **The Secret Path** — Gord Downie's multimedia project about Chanie Wenjack, a 12-year-old Anishinaabe boy who died fleeing a residential school
- **"I Lost My Talk"** — Rita Joe's iconic poem about the loss of language and culture
- **Truth and Reconciliation Commission (TRC)** — Findings, key recommendations, and the 94 Calls to Action
- **Sugar Falls** — A graphic novel based on the true story of a residential school survivor
- **The Lonely Death of Chanie Wenjack** — Case study examining the tragic consequences of the residential school system
- **Residential School Settlement Agreement** — The Indian Residential Schools Settlement Agreement (IRSSA) and its components
- **Life After Residential Schools** — Intergenerational trauma and the path to healing
- **Unit Test & Review** — Comprehensive assessment covering Treaties, Indian Act, Reserves, and Residential Schools

## Unit 11: Government

**Folder:** `11.  Government-20250611T175411Z-1-001`

### Overview
This unit examines Indigenous governance, the movement toward self-government, and the relationship between Indigenous peoples and the Crown. It also addresses the Sixties Scoop and life on reserves in contemporary Canada.

### Highlights
- **Self-Government & Sovereignty** — The evolution of Indigenous self-governance and treaty rights
- **The Sixties Scoop** — Cause and consequence of the mass removal of Indigenous children from their families into foster/adoption systems
- **Treaty Promises** — Rekindling Indigenous-Crown land relationships in Canada
- **Life on Reserves in Canada Today** — Contemporary challenges and realities
- **Kekinamuek: Learning About the Mi'kmaq** — Mi'kmaw-specific governance content
- **Governing a Nation** — Worksheet exploring how Indigenous nations governed themselves
- **Implementing Treaties and Government** — Practical application of treaty obligations

## Unit 13: Justice

**Folder:** `13. Justice-20250611T175151Z-1-001`

### Overview
This unit investigates the overrepresentation of Indigenous peoples in the Canadian criminal justice system, systemic injustices, and alternative justice models rooted in Indigenous traditions.

### Highlights
- **Justice Denied** — Case studies of wrongful convictions of Indigenous people in Canada (5 cases)
- **Aboriginal Justice & Injustices** — Comprehensive overview of systemic issues
- **First Nations in the Criminal Justice System** — Statistical analysis and root causes of overrepresentation
- **Sentencing Circles** — Restorative justice practices rooted in Indigenous traditions
- **Justice Organizer** — Interactive activity to explore themes of justice and injustice

## Unit 14: Current Issues

**Folder:** `14.  Current Issues-20250611T175352Z-1-001`

### Overview
This unit addresses contemporary issues facing Indigenous peoples, including the MMIWG crisis, media representation, UN declarations, and positive changes in Mi'kmaw communities.

### Highlights
- **MMIWG (Missing and Murdered Indigenous Women and Girls)** — National inquiry, videos, and findings
- **Sixties Scoop Timeline** — Historical timeline and survivor stories ("Forgotten Generation")
- **Uncontacted Tribes** — Global Indigenous issues, including the impact of modern technology (e.g., Elon Musk bringing internet to remote tribes)
- **The Pocahontas Paradox & Myth** — Deconstructing the Disney myth vs. the real historical figure; media representation of Indigenous peoples
- **Reel Injun** — Documentary analysis of Indigenous portrayals in film
- **UN Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples (UNDRIP)** — International framework for Indigenous rights
- **Beyond 94** — Tracking progress on the TRC's 94 Calls to Action
- **First Nations in the News Today** — Current events and ongoing issues
- **Change Has Started** — Positive developments and improvements in Mi'kmaw communities
