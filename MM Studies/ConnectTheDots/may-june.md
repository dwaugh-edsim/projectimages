## 🏆 Current Priority: TRC Bristolboard "Level-Up"
*Draeden is already working on this. Let's make it more interactive:*

1.  **Opinion Stickers**: Use 3 colors of stickers/markers. Green = "I see this at school", Yellow = "Super Important", Blue = "Needs Work." Mark 10 recommendations.
2.  **Visual Translation**: For his 3 favorite recommendations, draw a small sketch next to them showing it actually happening.
3.  **Pro Header**: Use the computer to design a bold title: "DRAEDEN’S TRC REPORT CARD." Print it and center it at the top of the board.
4.  **Sacred Teachings Tagging**: Use the 7 animals (Eagle, Bear, Turtle, etc.) to "tag" specific TRC recommendations. 
    *   Example: Put the **Turtle (Truth)** next to a recommendation about telling the true history. 
    *   Example: Put the **Bear (Courage)** next to a recommendation that will be hard for the government to fix.

## 🎨 Unit 10: Residential Schools (Days 1–7)
1.  **Chanie Wenjack’s Path (Map)**: Draw a map of Chanie's escape route based on the *Secret Path* video. Use 3 colors to show the weather, the tracks, and the shelter.
2.  **"We Were Children" Sketch**: Watch a 5-minute clip and draw one object from the video (e.g., a suitcase, a desk, a window). Write 3 words describing it.
3.  **The Secret Path Album Cover**: Design a new album cover for Gord Downie’s *Secret Path* that Draeden thinks looks "cool."
4.  **Language Treasure Map**: Find 5 Mi'kmaw words for family members. Draw a "treasure map" where these words are the locations.
5.  **Sugar Falls Visual Response**: Look at the graphic novel *Sugar Falls*. Choose one page and redraw it in Draeden's own style.
6.  **"I Lost My Talk" Acrostic**: Use the letters in "MI'KMAQ" to write 6 things Rita Joe wanted to keep.
7.  **Residential School Digital Tour**: Use Google Earth to find the location of a former residential school in the Maritimes. Take a screenshot and mark where the "playground" was.

## 🏛️ Unit 11: Government & Treaties (Days 8–12)
8.  **Treaty Medal Design**: Look at a historical Treaty Medal. Draw a modern version of it that represents "Peace and Friendship" today.
9.  **The Sixties Scoop Ticker**: Use a digital news ticker tool (or draw one) to create 3 short headlines about what the Sixties Scoop was.
10. **Community Map**: Create a color-coded map of a local Mi'kmaw community (like Membertou or Sipekne'katik). Label the Band Office and the School.
11. **Chief for a Day**: Draw a picture of Draeden as a Chief. List 3 things he would do to help his community.
12. **The "Self-Gov" Symbol**: Design a simple logo or flag that represents Mi'kmaw Self-Government.

## ✊ Unit 12: Protest & Resistance (Days 13–15)
13. **Protest Poster**: Create a bold, visual poster for "One Dead Indian" (Dudley George). Focus on one word (e.g., JUSTICE).
14. **Timeline of Action**: Draw a simple timeline with 3 dates showing Indigenous people standing up for their rights.
15. **Ipperwash Map**: Draw a map of Ipperwash Park. Use a different color to show where the protesters were.

## ⚖️ Unit 13: Justice & Sentencing Circles (Days 16–20)
16. **The Justice Circle**: Draw a "Sentencing Circle." Place 4 people in the circle and label who they are (Judge, Victim, Offender, Community Member).
17. **Wrongful Conviction Card**: Create a "Fact Card" for one of the 5 case studies. Draw the person's face and write why they were innocent.
18. **Traditional vs Modern Justice**: Draw a scale. On one side, draw a "Handcuff" (Modern). On the other, draw a "Feather" (Traditional). 
19. **Circle of Healing**: Draw a circle and fill it with 5 things that make someone feel better after they've been hurt.
20. **Justice Emoji Set**: Create 5 emojis that represent different feelings about the Justice unit.

## 🌐 Unit 14: Current Issues (Days 21–25)
21. **MMIWG Commemorative Ribbon**: Design a red dress or ribbon symbol. Write one name or message inside it.
22. **The Pocahontas Myth (Digital)**: Compare a screenshot of Disney's Pocahontas with a picture of the real Sarah (Sarah Family History). Write "True" or "Myth" under 3 differences.
23. **Media Watch**: Find one news article about a Mi'kmaw person today. Draw a "TV Screen" and sketch what the news reporter is showing.
24. **Future Community**: Draw what a Mi'kmaw community might look like in the year 2050.
25. **"Beyond 94" Badge**: Create a "Challenge Badge" for one of the TRC Calls to Action.
