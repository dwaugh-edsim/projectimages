Office of the Supervising Coroner

Inquest into the death of: Chanie (Charlie) Wenjack

Case No: 1966-441-CW

Lead Investigator:

EXHIBIT A: THE INCIDENT

On October 23, 1966, the body of Chanie Wenjack was found beside the CN Railway tracks. He was 600km away from home. He was wearing only a light cotton jacket. He had seven matches in his pocket.

Investigator's Query: Why was he so far from home? What was the primary obstacle between him and survival?

EXHIBIT B: THE LEGAL CHAIN

Chanie was taken from his family under an amendment to the Indian Act (1920). This law made it a criminal offense for parents to keep their children home from school.

Investigator's Query: Who is legally responsible for Chanie being 600km away from home? Defend your answer.

EXHIBIT C: THE JUROR'S VERDICT

The 1966 jury stated: "The Indian problem is not a problem of the Indian, but of the white man."

Final Deliberation: Chanie was only 12. Why would he attempt a 600km walk in a cotton jacket? What was he running from? Use your findings from the 'Connect the Dots' timeline to support your conclusion.
