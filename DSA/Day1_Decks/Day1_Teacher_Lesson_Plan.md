## 🎯 Pedagogical Philosophy: AI-Proof & Zero Paper

### Why These Tasks Cannot Be AI-Generated:
1. **Live Device Telemetry (HL9):** Students pull actual screen time, pickup counts, and morning app data from their physical pocket devices. AI cannot fabricate their real hardware statistics.
2. **Physical Kinesthetic Debate (CIT9):** 4-Corners movement forces students to take a visible, physical stance in the room and defend it verbally on the spot.
3. **Peer Cross-Examination (Both):** Live 1-on-1 verbal interrogation with mandatory counter-questions eliminates Chromebook copy-paste and ChatGPT auto-completions.
4. **Physical Whiteboard Artifacts:** Class data is aggregated via live tallies and sticky notes on the classroom board to build our actual room constitution.
