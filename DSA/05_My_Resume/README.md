﻿My Resume Assignment


A resume is a document that people put together to try and gain employment. A resume acts as a summary of your skills, education and qualifications and is usually presented in a typed one-page document. For this assignment, you will be creating your own resume for your grade 8 year.












I Can 
Statements
	Complete the I can statements below to ensure that you have completed this assignment correctly:


* I can complete the Reflection Activity on page two and reflect honestly on the questions asked 
* I can complete the resume template on page three
* I can submit the assignment when it is completed to our Google classroom
	Reflection Activity
	The reflection activity is designed to get you thinking about what you might want to include on your resume.
	

Resume Template


	After completing the reflection activity it is now time to complete the Resume Template. This will act as your final copy and the resume you can use on your student council application. Here is what you will need to include for each section:


* Profile
   * A quick two facts about you that you want the reader to know about you
* Skills
   * List three skills that you think you are good at
* References
   * Who are two members of the DSA community that you feel can speak about you?
	

Outcomes


	Outcomes covered: Learners will formulate responses to change in Canadian Society.


Learners will analyze how skills influence physical, mental, emotional, social, and spiritual health.


Competencies: Citizenship, Creativity and Innovation, Critical Thinking, Technological Fluency, Personal Career Development
	

Reflection Activity


Before creating your Resume it is important to think about and reflect back on some of your past experiences to think about the skills and strengths that you have. Complete the questions below in as much detail as possible.
