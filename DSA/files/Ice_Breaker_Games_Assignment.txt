Ice Breaker Assignment


Icebreakers are games used to help people get to know one another. These types of games can be a great way to introduce people who are coming together for the first time. For this assignment you will be responsible for creating or finding an Icebreaker game to present to a younger group of students. Use the guide below to complete this assignment.








I Can
	To complete this assignment please follow the “I can” statements listed below:


I can create my own Icebreaker game or remix a created Icebreaker game


I can create my own resources to go with the Icebreaker game


I can present my Icebreaker game to a younger group of students within our school community


I can submit any resources I have created for this to our Google Classroom
	

Creating Your Resource
	Create a resource that can be used to help explain your Icebreaker game. Your resource should include the following:


* Basic Description of the Icebreaker Game
   * Provide a basic description of the Icebreaker game you selected or created. 
   * Did you create the Icebreaker or did you remix an Icebreaker that was already created?
* Rules and How to Play
   * Here you will outline how to play the game and any rules the players must be aware of.
   * Are there any hazards players must look out for or be careful of while playing?
* Sportsmanship and Community:
   * Define the two terms: Sportsmanship and Community
   * Try and relate the game back to one of our four core community values respect, kindness, honesty, empathy.
   * Complete this reflection question:
      * Provide two examples of positive and negative sportsmanship
      * How can Icebreaker games help create a greater sense of community?
	



Presentation


	For this assignment, you will be responsible for deciding how to present your information. Here are some potential formats:
* Google Docs
* Google Slides
* We Video
* CoSpaces, Minecraft etc
* Talk to me if you have an idea
	

Outcomes
	

Learners will reflect on how life skills impact emotional, physical, social, emotional and spiritual health.
