Anchor Chart Assignment


The right anchor chart can be a valuable tool in a classroom. Anchor charts provide us with important information about processes, and procedures and helpful information on important skills. For this assignment, you will be tasked with creating an anchor chart on a topic that you think would be helpful for our learning environment. See the instructions below to complete this assignment.










I Can Statements
	Follow the “I can” statements below to complete this assignment:


I can select a topic to create an anchor chart on


I can create an anchor chart that highlights the key information on the topic I selected


I can be creative and complete my anchor chart on paper or digitally online

Design Process 
	This project can be completed digitally or on paper. If you are going to attempt it digitally programs like Canva or Google Slides would probably work best.


If you are attempting this on paper I would plan it out on normal blank paper before trying to do it on a larger sheet.
