Bucci’s Class Assessment Rubric
When it comes to marking assignments and quizzes please refer to this rubric to get a better understanding of what I am looking for and what you will need to demonstrate for each level. Every assignment and quiz will come with its own set of instructions so this rubric is just a general overview. I will also consult with the  Atlantic Learning Framework.


Level
	Criteria / Expectations
	

4
	Here is what I am looking for when it comes to my grading rubric for a level 4:


* Quizzes
   * All questions are answered in GREAT detail with the information from the readings
   * Information is factually correct
   * Information is presented in complete sentences with the use of proper grammar.
   * Students are able to reflect on and demonstrate their understanding of content
* Projects and Assignments
   * Students follow the “I Can” statements from the assignment outline and include the required information from the assignment
   * Information that is used and presented is factually correct and properly sourced (No Plagiarism)
   * Information is presented in a clear and creative way that demonstrates the students understanding of the subject matter 
	

3
	Here is what I am looking for when it comes to my grading rubric for a level 3:
* Quizzes
   * All questions are answered in GOOD detail with the information from the readings
   * Information is MOSTLY factually correct
   * Information is presented in complete sentences with SOME MINOR GRAMMATICAL ERRORS. 
   * Students are able to reflect on and demonstrate their understanding of content
* Projects and Assignments
   * Students follow the “I Can” statements from the assignment outline and include the required information from the assignment
   * Information that is used and presented is factually correct and properly sourced (No Plagiarism)
   * Information is presented in a clear and creative way that demonstrates the students understanding of the subject matter
	

2
	Here is what I am looking for when it comes to my grading rubric for a level 2:
* Quizzes
   * SOME questions are answered in SATISFACTORY detail with the information from the readings
   * Information is factually INCORRECT
   * Information is presented in point form notes and there are lots of spelling and grammatical errors.
   * Students are not able to demonstrate an understanding of the subject matter.
* Projects and Assignments
   * Students doesn't follow most of the “I Can” statements from the assignment outline and include the required information from the assignment
   * Information that is used and presented is factually incorrect and is not properly sourced (No Plagiarism)
   * Information is not presented in a clear and creative way that demonstrates the students understanding of the subject matter
	1
	Here is what I am looking for when it comes to my grading rubric for a level 1:
* Quizzes
   * Not completed or attempted
   * Completed one question in class and did not submit the rest
* Projects and Assignments
   * Not working on the activity during the time given in class
   * Not following the information from the assignment 
   * Not turning in anything to our Google Classroom
