### Breakfast Club Community Service Project
- **Downloads:** [`Breakfast_Club_Community_Service_Project.pdf`](../files/Breakfast_Club_Community_Service_Project.pdf) | [`Breakfast_Club_Community_Service_Project.docx`](../files/Breakfast_Club_Community_Service_Project.docx)

#### Assignment Instructions / Content:
```text
﻿Breakfast Club Community Service Project 


The Breakfast Program plays an important role within our community ensuring that everyone has access to a healthy breakfast. At Dartmouth South, the Breakfast Program is looking for students who are serious about making a positive impact on their community and who are looking to gain some leadership experience and community service hours. Interested in taking part in the Health Project? See the details below.








I Can
	Follow these “I Can” statements to ensure that you successfully complete this project:


I can assist with the Breakfast Program during the designated time periods 


I can complete the community service hours log sheet


I can complete reflections after taking part in a skills class or running a hot breakfast 
	





Breakfast Program Needs
	We are looking for students to volunteer during one lunch a week and for their designated ILB period. Students will be taking part in the following:


* Helping to package and prepare for future breakfasts 
* Learning skills to help with preparing for hot breakfasts
* Helping to raise awareness for the Breakfast Program 
	Community Service Hours Log Sheet
	

Each week make sure you sign in to the Breakfast Club sign-in sheet. This sheet will be used to calculate the total hours you have  
	





Reflection
	After taking part in any kind of skills class or preparing and helping to run a hot breakfast you will be asked to complete a reflection about that experience. Here are some things to keep in mind when writing your reflection:


* How do you think things went?
* What do you think went well? 
* What do you think did not go well?
* What would you do differently next time?                
	

Outcomes 
	Learners will implement an advocacy action in response to an issue. 


Learners will learn how life skills influence physical, mental, emotional, social and spiritual health


Students who complete over 15 hours of community services and the other components of this project will receive a reference letter at the end of the project stating their role and the hours they complete with the club.
```

### Intramural League Assignment
- **Downloads:** [`Intramural_League_Assignment.pdf`](../files/Intramural_League_Assignment.pdf) | [`Intramural_League_Assignment.docx`](../files/Intramural_League_Assignment.docx)

#### Assignment Instructions / Content:
```text
﻿Intramural League Project


        Intramural sports leagues are a great way of building a sense of community and encouraging a healthy active lifestyle. For this assignment, you are going to be responsible for creating and organizing your own Intramural League. Follow the instructions below to complete this assignment.












I Can
Statements
	Follow the “I can” statements below to complete this assignment.


I can select a sport or esport and create a three-week Intramural League 


I can ensure that my projects have all of the required information


I can create and publish my Intramural League Project in the form of a google site




	









Required Information
	For this assignment, you are going to need to focus on planning and displaying the following information:


* Basic Information
   * What is the sport 
   * Name of the league
   * How long the league will run for 
   * Basic rules and how the league will run
* Organize teams
   * Create a way for students interested in signing up to sign up so you can make teams (sign up sheet, create a Google form)
   * Create fair teams based on the sign-up interests
* Create a schedule 
   * Ensure that every team gets a chance to participate and take part in the league.


	



Presentation
	This project will be presented on Google Sites so that students and teachers who are taking part in this Intramural league can know what team they are on when they play and the results about who won or lost. You will need to create Google Docs to hold your important information and you can embed them into your league website.
	

Outcomes
	Outcomes covered: Learners will formulate responses to change in Canadian Society.


Learners will analyze how skills influence physical, mental, emotional, social, and spiritual health.
```

﻿School Spirit Exploration


        This exploration is designed to help you explore, educate and unite your school community. Brainstorm, design, reflect and execute a plan to create an experience that unites and promotes the values of our school community. Follow the guide below to complete this exploration.










I Can
	Follow the “I can” statements below to ensure that you successfully complete this exploration:


* I can brainstorm and come up with a proposal for an event or experience to organize at DSA 
* I can come up with five questions of my own about the potential risks and benefits of my proposal
* I can present my information in a clear and creative way.
* I can document my process of completing this assignment in My Blueprint Portfolio


	



















Community Event 






	When it comes to deciding on a potential project idea for this exploration it is important that whatever you decide to do aligns with what you are interested in. Here are some potential ideas you could explore:


* Spirit Week (Past Example)
   * Brainstorm potential themes for spirit week days, gauge the interest of the school community by creating a survey, analyze and reflect on the results, and then finally create a media campaign that educates the school community on the results and dates of spirit week.
* School Newspaper
   * Work with your peers to explore what is going on within our school community and then present your findings. Create a plan for the first issue and then carry it out.
* Plan A Dance
   * Select a school-appropriate theme for the dance, create a potential playlist and brainstorm potential decoration options.
* Have an idea for a project? Speak to me and we can try and figure it out together!
	



Presentation
	You can decide on how you are going to go about presenting your information. You just need to ensure that it is submitted to our Google Classroom. Here are some potential formats to present your information: 
* Poster
* Essay
* Google Slides
* Create a Website
* Digital Model on CoSpaces, Minecraft, or Scratch
* We Video Project (Movies, podcast, GIFS, Animation)
