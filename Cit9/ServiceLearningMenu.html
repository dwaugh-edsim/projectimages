Unit 1: Engaged Citizenship

The 7-Day Action Challenge

Service learning isn't just about knowing; it's about doing. Choose your issue, research the impact, and change your life for one week.

Note on Collaborative Impact: Usually, we want unique projects. However, for this challenge, overlap is encouraged. Multiple students choosing the same challenge (e.g., 10 people doing the Battery Scavenger) allows for a massive Collective Impact Report during the final assessment phase.

01. The Battery Scavenger

SDG 12: Consumption

The Investigation

Research the toxicity of lithium in HRM landfills and the ethics of cobalt mining in the DRC.

The 7-Day Challenge

Find every dead battery in your home. Safely tape the ends and deliver them to a certified recycler.

02. The Tap Audit

SDG 06: Clean Water

The Investigation

Research the "SDG in our Backyard"—the persistence of boil-water advisories in Indigenous communities.

The 7-Day Challenge

Reduce personal water usage by 25%. Time your showers, stop the tap while brushing, and audit every drop.

03. The Phantom Load

SDG 07: Clean Energy

The Investigation

Investigate the "Phantom Load"—the electricity used by devices that are plugged in but turned off.

The 7-Day Challenge

Unplug every "vampire" device in your bedroom (chargers, screens, lamps) every night for 7 nights.

04. The Wardrobe Reset

SDG 12: Consumption

The Investigation

Research the global labor ethics of "Fast Fashion" and the volume of textile waste in Canada.

The 7-Day Challenge

Select only 10 items of clothing. Wear only those items for 7 days to protest disposable fashion.

05. The Data Diet

SDG 16: Peace & Justice

The Investigation

Investigate global data privacy rights and how your "Digital Shadow" is harvested by corporations.

The 7-Day Challenge

Disable all non-essential tracking and notifications on your phone. Reclaim your digital footprint for a week.

06. The zero-waste Audit

SDG 13: Climate Action

The Investigation

Research the lifecycle of single-use plastics and their impact on global ocean health.

The 7-Day Challenge

Zero single-use plastics in your personal meals. No wrappers, no bottles, no straws for 7 days.

Step 04: The Assessment — At the end of the week, students must collaboratively analyze the data from their challenges to prove the collective impact of these individual actions.
