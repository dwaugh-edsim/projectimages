Outcome: Engaged Citizenship

Beyond the Poster:
The Service Learning Action Menu

For the Gr 9 Service Learning Project, research is just the fuel. The goal is construction—collaboratively designing a local solution to a global challenge.

The "Presentation" Trap

Traditional projects end with a student presentation. In Citizenship 9, the presentation is a Launch. We aren't just talking about issues; we are constructing the systems to address them.

The Goal: Construction

Every group must move from Investigation (in-class research) to Collaborative Design (building the station, the toolkit, or the advocacy framework).

Select Your Global-Local Investigation Arc

01. The Toxic Hoard

SDG 12: Responsible Consumption

The Investigation

Research the humanitarian cost of Cobalt/Lithium mining in the DRC and the toxicity of e-waste in Canadian landfills.

The Construction

Design and build a permanent "Battery Diversion Lab" station for the school foyer.

02. The Thirsty Communities

SDG 06: Clean Water

The Investigation

Research global water privatization vs. the persistence of boil-water advisories in Canadian Indigenous communities.

The Construction

Construct a "School Hydration Audit" and design permanent high-impact signage for school refill stations to reduce plastic waste.

03. The Fast Fashion Lifecycle

SDG 12: Responsible Consumption

The Investigation

Investigate the labor ethics of the global garment industry and the environmental impact of textile waste.

The Construction

Construct a "Lost & Found Recovery System"—sorting existing textile waste and designing a one-day pop-up redistribution event.

04. The Digital Shadow

SDG 16: Peace & Justice

The Investigation

Investigate the ethics of mass data harvesting, algorithmic bias, and the erosion of digital privacy rights globally.

The Construction

Construct a "Digital Privacy Awareness Kit"—including physical webcam covers and data-safety posters for the computer lab.

05. The Calories & Carbon Audit

SDG 02: Zero Hunger

The Investigation

Research the carbon footprint of global food logistics and the ethics of migrant labor in international agriculture.

The Construction

Construct a "School Food-Mile Map" for the cafeteria and draft a formal proposal for a monthly "100-Mile Meal" day.
