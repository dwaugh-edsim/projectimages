## Discussion Guide: Equity Angle
### Who Benefits from Solutions and Who Gets Left Behind

**Case Study 1: Ozone Hole Recovery (Global Solution)**
- The Montreal Protocol (1987) phased out CFCs globally
- Developed countries had resources to switch to alternatives quickly
- Developing countries got financial assistance and longer timelines
- Result: Global cooperation with equity considerations built in

**Case Study 2: Leaded Gasoline Phaseout (US First, Global Later)**
- US began reduction in 1973, full ban by 1996
- Many developing countries continued using leaded gasoline into 2000s
- Algeria was last to phase it out in 2021
- Result: US children saw dramatic blood lead improvements decades before Global South

## Rubric: Assessment Criteria
### For Risk Check, News Audit, and Post-Mortem Assignments

**4-Point Scale for Each Criterion:**

**Evidence Use:**
1 (Anecdotal): Relies on personal stories, social media, unverified claims
2 (Selective): Uses some data but ignores contradictory evidence
3 (Comprehensive): Uses multiple reliable sources, checks claims thoroughly
4 (Insightful synthesis): Combines evidence creatively, identifies patterns/gaps

**Source Quality:**
1 (Unreliable): Blogs without expertise, social media rumors, unverified claims
2 (Mixed): Some reliable sources but also questionable ones
3 (Credible): Government data, peer-reviewed research, established news orgs
4 (Exceptional): Triangulates sources, uses primary data, assesses methodology

**Reasoning:**
1 (Emotional): Conclusions based on feelings, fears, hopes rather than data
2 (Logical): Clear connection between evidence and conclusion
3 (Nuanced): Recognizes complexity, limitations, multiple factors
4 (Recognizes complexity and agency): Sees both structural factors and human choice

**Solution Orientation (for applicable assignments):**
1 (Hopeless): Sees only problems, no paths forward
2 (Aware of problems): Can describe issues but no action ideas
3 (Sees levers for change): Identifies specific actions that could help
4 (Identifies actionable steps): Provides concrete, feasible steps to take

**Citizenship Engagement:**
1 (Passive observer): Just describes what's happening
2 (Concerned): Expresses worry but no sense of efficacy
3 (Informed): Understands issue and knows where to find reliable info
4 (Ready to act): Has specific plan for engagement or advocacy
