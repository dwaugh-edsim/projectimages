# UN Sustainable Development Goals Summary The Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), also known as the Global Goals, were adopted by the United Nations in 2015 as a universal call to action to end poverty, protect the planet, and ensure that by 2030 all people enjoy peace and prosperity. ## The 17 Goals 1. **No Poverty** Eradicate extreme poverty and ensure social protection for all vulnerable populations. This goal aims to provide equal access to economic resources and basic services globally. 2. **Zero Hunger** End hunger and malnutrition by ensuring everyone has access to safe, nutritious, and sufficient food year-round. It also promotes sustainable agriculture and supports small-scale food producers. 3. **Good Health and Well-being** Ensure healthy lives and promote well-being for people of all ages through improved healthcare access. Key targets include reducing maternal and child mortality and ending epidemics like AIDS and malaria. 4. **Quality Education** Ensure inclusive and equitable quality education and promote lifelong learning opportunities for all. This includes providing free primary and secondary education and affordable vocational training. 5. **Gender Equality** Achieve gender equality and empower all women and girls by eliminating discrimination and violence. It focuses on ensuring equal participation in leadership and access to economic and natural resources. 6. **Clean Water and Sanitation** Ensure availability and sustainable management of water and sanitation for all people. This goal aims to improve water quality, increase water-use efficiency, and protect water-related ecosystems. 7. **Affordable and Clean Energy** Ensure access to affordable, reliable, sustainable, and modern energy for everyone. It promotes increasing the share of renewable energy and improving energy efficiency worldwide. 8. **Decent Work and Economic Growth** Promote sustained, inclusive, and sustainable economic growth and productive employment. This involves achieving higher levels of economic productivity and ensuring decent work for all women and men. 9. **Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure** Build resilient infrastructure, promote inclusive and sustainable industrialization, and foster innovation. It emphasizes increasing access to information and communication technology and supporting small-scale enterprises. 10. **Reduced Inequalities** Reduce inequality within and among countries by promoting social, economic, and political inclusion for all. This goal aims to ensure equal opportunity and reduce inequalities of outcome. 11. **Sustainable Cities and Communities** Make cities and human settlements inclusive, safe, resilient, and sustainable. Key targets include ensuring access to safe and affordable housing and sustainable transport systems. 12. **Responsible Consumption and Production** Ensure sustainable consumption and production patterns by reducing waste and improving resource efficiency. It encourages businesses and consumers to adopt sustainable practices and reduce their environmental footprint. 13. **Climate Action** Take urgent action to combat climate change and its impacts through mitigation and adaptation strategies. This goal focuses on strengthening resilience and integrating climate measures into national policies. 14. **Life Below Water** Conserve and sustainably use the oceans, seas, and marine resources for sustainable development. It aims to prevent marine pollution and protect marine and coastal ecosystems. 15. **Life on Land** Protect, restore, and promote sustainable use of terrestrial ecosystems and halt biodiversity loss. This includes managing forests sustainably, combating desertification, and protecting endangered species. 16. **Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions** Promote peaceful and inclusive societies and provide access to justice for all. It focuses on building effective, accountable, and inclusive institutions at all levels. 17. **Partnerships for the Goals** Strengthen the means of implementation and revitalize the global partnership for sustainable development. This goal emphasizes international cooperation, trade, and technology sharing to achieve all other SDGs. --- ### References 1.
