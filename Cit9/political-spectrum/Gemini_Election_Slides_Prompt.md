# Halifax West Election 2026: Campaign Trail Slide Deck Prompt

*You can copy and paste the text below directly into Google Gemini (or use it as an outline in Google Slides/Canva) to generate the presentation for the class.*

---

**Prompt for Gemini:**
Please generate a 7-slide presentation deck outline for a high school Citizenship 9 class participating in an election simulation. Use a professional, high-energy, exciting "election night" tone. Here is the content for each slide:

**Slide 1: Title Slide**
*   **Title:** Welcome to the Halifax West 2026 Campaign!
*   **Subtitle:** The War Room is closed. The Campaign Trail begins.
*   **Visual Suggestion:** A vibrant map of Halifax or a modern election ballot graphic.

**Slide 2: The Tournament Structure**
*   **Heading:** The Rules of the Game
*   **Bullet Points:**
    *   **Phase 1: The Primary:** All parties pitch their platforms. The class votes. The Top 4 advance.
    *   **Phase 2: The Town Hall:** The Final 4 Leaders face the Press Gallery in a live debate.
    *   **Phase 3: General Election:** Final secret ballot. You cannot vote for your own party!

**Slide 3: Choose Your Role**
*   **Heading:** How will you win? (Groups of 2 or 3)
*   **Bullet Points:**
    *   🎤 **The Party Leader:** Writes and delivers the 2-minute "Launch Speech." You are the face of the party.
    *   📊 **The Campaign Manager:** Builds and operates the 3-Slide presentation (Title, Policies, Budget).
    *   🛡️ **The Policy Strategist:** The "Attack Dog." You will use the *Opposition Research Hub* to dig up dirt and ask the tough questions.

**Slide 4: Deep Dive - The Primary Pitches**
*   **Heading:** Round 1: The Primary
*   **Description:** Every single party will take the stage. You have precisely **2 minutes** to deliver your Launch Speech and show your presentation slides. No questions, no interruptions.
*   **The Cut:** After every party speaks, we vote. The Top 4 survive.

**Slide 5: Deep Dive - The Final Four Town Hall**
*   **Heading:** Round 2: The Town Hall
*   **Description:** The 4 surviving Leaders take chairs at the front of the room.
*   **The Press Gallery:** If your party was eliminated, you become an Investigative Journalist. Using your pre-written attack questions, you will ruthlessly grill the Final 4 on their budgets and policies.

**Slide 6: The Tools**
*   **Heading:** Your Campaign Toolkit
*   **Bullet Points:**
    *   📝 **Google Docs:** For drafting your 2-minute script.
    *   🎨 **Canva or Google Slides:** To build your 3-4 slide visual presentation.
    *   💻 **The Opposition Research Hub:** Our custom web app. Use it to read rival platforms and write your final attack questions!

**Slide 7: Your Mission TODAY (Action Slide)**
*   **Heading:** TODAY'S OBJECTIVES
*   **Bullet 1:** **LEADERS:** Open a Google Doc. Start converting your platform into a 2-minute speech ending with your slogan.
*   **Bullet 2:** **MANAGERS:** Open Canva/Slides. Build Slide 1 (Logo/Names), Slide 2 (Top 3 Policies), Slide 3 (The Budget/Funding).
*   **Bullet 3:** **STRATEGISTS:** Open the *Opposition Research Hub*. Find two rival parties and write out down one devastating (but respectful) question for the debate.
*   **Banner:** You have the rest of the class. Get to work!
