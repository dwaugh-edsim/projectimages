What is Service Learning?

Research & Investigation

We don't just "do things." We investigate WHY they matter. We look at the data, the laws, and the global supply chains.

Direct Action

We solve real problems in our community. We measure the weight of the waste we divert or the litres of water we save.

Collaborative Design

This isn't a preset assignment. We are building this project TOGETHER based on your interests and skills.

Merit & Evaluation

We assess the merit of our choices. Does this project actually help? Is the impact sustainable?

Our Goals Today

We are designing the 14-Day Action Challenge. I need your intelligence on 3 points:

  • 1. Preference: Which of these would you actually pick?
  • 2. Feasibility: Which deliverables seem like "too much"?
  • 3. Innovation: Do you have a better idea?

CIT-001: The Battery Scavenger

SDG 12 E-Waste

The Diversion Lab

Old batteries sitting in drawers leach toxins into our soil. Recycling them reduces the demand for unethical mining in the DRC.

Deliverables: Home Battery Audit, Multi-meter Voltage Logs, Supply Chain Map, 1-Page Diversion Report.

The Mission

Set up a classroom collection station. Tape terminals, sort chemistries, and deliver to a recycler.

Key Challenge: Managing the "Thermal Runaway" fire risk of lithium-ion cells.

CIT-002: The Tap Audit

SDG 06 Indigenous Rights

Water Privilege

Measure the gap between municipal luxury in Halifax and the 25+ year boil-water advisories in First Nations communities.

Deliverables: 14-Day Water Usage Spreadsheet, Indigenous Community Profile, Water Privilege Infographic.

The Mission

Reduce your personal water footprint by 25% for 14 days. Time every shower, audit every drop.

Key Challenge: Living with restricted access while researching systemic infrastructure gaps.

CIT-003: The Phantom Load

SDG 07 Carbon Footprint

Energy Vampire Hunt

Unplugged devices draw 10% of your home's power. In NS, that power often comes from burning coal.

Deliverables: Room-by-Room Phantom Audit, Cost/Carbon Spreadsheet, Before/After Power Photos.

The Mission

Identify every "always-on" device. Unplug non-essentials every night for 14 nights to reclaim your footprint.

Key Challenge: Negotiating energy habits with your family and measuring coal-based carbon waste.

CIT-004: The Wardrobe Reset

SDG 12 Labour Rights

The True Cost of $5

Fast fashion relies on unethical labour in the global south. A single pair of jeans takes 7,500L of water to make.

Deliverables: Full Wardrobe Inventory, 10-Item Capsule Log, Rana Plaza Case Study & Supply Chain Map.

The Mission

Select only 10 items of clothing. Wear ONLY those items for 14 days to protest disposable fashion.

Key Challenge: Resisting the "need" for variety to understand the environmental cost of fashion.

CIT-005: The Data Diet

SDG 16 Digital Rights

Shadow Profiles

Data brokers track you even without accounts. Algorithms create echo chambers that distort democratic reality.

Deliverables: App Privacy Audit, Tracker-Blocker Log, Bill C-27 Analysis, Data Diet Reflection.

The Mission

Purge non-essential tracking. 24-hour social media notification fast. Reclaim your digital autonomy.

Key Challenge: Breaking the "Phone-Reach" habit and understanding privacy law.

CIT-006: The Zero-Waste Audit

SDG 13 Global Ethics

Waste Colonialism

Only 9% of plastic is recycled. The rest is often shipped to developing nations as "Waste Colonialism."

Deliverables: Daily Plastic Pile Inventory, Zero-Plastic Substitution Log, Systemic Responsibility Analysis.
🌊

The Mission

Go zero single-use plastic for 14 days. If you can't reuse it, refuse it. Audit the grocery store aisles.

Key Challenge: Finding alternatives in a system designed for disposable convenience.

Assessment: Too much work?

We want this to be impact-heavy but not "busy work." Look at these 4 expected deliverables:

The Current List

  • Supply Chain Map: Tracing raw materials to retail.
  • 14-Day Action Log: Daily evidence/photos of your work.
  • Research Dossier: 4 deep-dive questions with citations.
  • Consequence Report: Final data-driven reflection.

Student Assessment:

  • Which of these feels like "busy work"?
  • How can we simplify these while keeping the impact?
  • Is 14 days the right duration?

Innovation: Better Ideas?

Starter Ideas to Spark Your Brain:

  • The Repair Protocol: Fix one thing instead of binning it.
  • The Food Miles Audit: Mapping the journey of your lunch.
  • The Digital Minimalism: 14 days of "Attention Autonomy."
