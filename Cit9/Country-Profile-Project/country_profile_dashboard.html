Country Profile Project

Global Citizenship Country Profile Project

PROJECT OVERVIEW

In pairs, you will create a slideshow presentation profiling a selected country (other than Canada), focused on its role regarding one specific global issue. You'll investigate how this country has responded or failed to respond and evaluate the consequences of those actions.

Task Description: Required Slideshow Sections

  • Introduction:
    • Country name
    • Brief background (geography, culture, language - first section of Wikipedia)
  • Selected Global Issue:
    • Clearly describe the issue (e.g., climate change, human rights, poverty, conflict)
    • Explain why this issue matters globally
  • Country's Actions or Inaction:
    • Describe how the country has responded or failed to respond to the issue
  • Consequences:
    • Discuss intended and unintended consequences of these actions or inactions
  • Comparison with Canada:
    • Briefly compare your country's response to Canada's actions on this same issue
  • Reflection:
    • Why should Canadians care about this issue?

Guidelines

  • Your slideshow should contain 6-8 slides
  • Use concise language and relevant visuals
  • Cite at least 2 credible sources at the bottom of each slide

Evaluation Criteria

1. Clear Introduction

Country and global issue are clearly introduced with relevant background information.

2. Analysis of Actions & Consequences

Thorough analysis of how the country has responded (or not) and the resulting consequences.

3. Comparison to Canada

Meaningful comparison between your country's response and Canada's actions on the same issue.

4. Thoughtful Reflection

Clear explanation of why Canadians should care about this issue.

5. Organization & Clarity

Well-organized slideshow with concise language, relevant visuals, and proper citations.
