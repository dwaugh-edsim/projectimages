# 🔍 Citizenship 9: Global Citizenship Scavenger Hunt

**Objective:** Explore the investigative projects created by your classmates to understand how global issues affect our local communities in Nova Scotia and Canada.

### 📝 Instructions for Students:
1.  **Explore the Gallery:** Use the [Project Gallery](index.html) to browse the student websites. 
2.  **Find the Answers:** Below are 30 questions derived from the research your classmates conducted.
3.  **Document Your Findings:** For each answer, you must:
    *   Provide the correct answer.
    *   **Cite the Source:** State the name of the project where you found the information.
4.  **Submission:** Create a Google Doc or a table to record your answers.

---

### 🕵️ The Scavenger Hunt Questions

1.  Approximately how many Nova Scotian children are currently living in households below the low-income threshold?
2.  By what percentage did Nova Scotian poverty rates decrease between the years 2000 and 2015?
3.  According to one project's projection, what could the poverty rate in NS rise to by the year 2046 if government funding was removed?
4.  Which two Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) are directly linked to the "Living Wage vs. Minimum Wage" debate?
5.  What does "Target 1.4" of the first Global Goal state should be achieved for all men and women by 2030?
6.  In the context of local employment, what does the "Cold Ramen" problem specifically refer to?
7.  How many years have some Indigenous communities in Canada been living under "boil water" orders?
8.  Which Global Goal focuses specifically on "Clean Water and Sanitation"?
9.  List three things that clean water is essential for, according to the project on water advisories.
10. In which two regions of the Maritimes does "ghost gear" (lost fishing equipment) appear most frequently?
11. How many tonnes of ghost gear have been removed from Canadian waters in total since the removal program began?
12. How many tonnes of gear were removed in a *single year* from the waters specifically near Nova Scotia?
13. On June 15, 2023, the government announced a $3.2 million investment. What specific age group is this mental health funding for?
14. At what specific age range do the projects suggest teens mostly experience the onset of mental health problems?
15. One project uses a "math lesson" scenario to describe feeling unsupported. What specific phrase are students sometimes told that makes them feel "invisible"?
16. How many people were evacuated from Kashechewan First Nation to Kingston, Ontario, due to a water system failure?
17. What is the primary aim of "Bill C-61"?
18. How many long-term drinking water advisories currently exist across the 13 First Nations in Nova Scotia?
19. What percentage of wastewater systems nationwide still fail to meet effluent regulations?
20. At what age did student Lachlan McMahon start his job at the Canada Games Centre?
21. How much money has the government set aside for "work-integrated learning opportunities" between 2026 and 2029?
22. What is the specific age range of young people helped by the Youth Employment and Skills Strategy (YESS)?
23. What was the reported unemployment rate for people aged 13-24 in Canada? (Compare this to the UK or China if you can!)
24. Which UN Global Goal (SDG) is focused on "Life Below Water"?
25. According to the "Timeline" on the lobster industry project, what major shift is expected to happen by the year 2035?
26. To which province are lobsters expected to migrate as ocean temperatures continue to rise?
27. Who is Angelique Cishayo, and how many years did she spend adapting to life in Canada?
28. By the year 2038, how much of an increase is expected in the annual amount of areas burned by wildfires?
29. Who is Larry Walker, and what harrowing experience did he have while evacuating Upper Tantallon?
30. List three specific health risks that are increased by exposure to wildfire smoke.
