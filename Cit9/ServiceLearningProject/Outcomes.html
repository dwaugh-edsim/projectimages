1. Curriculum Outcome

Engaged Citizenship Learners will construct a collaboratively-designed service learning project which addresses a need in the school or larger community.

This Service Learning Project is the culminating performance task for Citizenship 9. Students work in teams of 2–4 to identify a real community need, research its systemic roots, plan and execute a 14-day action response, and reflect on personal growth as citizens.

2. Indicators at a Glance

4. Five-Phase Workflow

The student workspace (File.html) walks each team through five sequential phases. Each phase produces evidence that maps directly to one or more indicators.

Step 1

Plan

Mission choice, why-it-matters rationale, personal strengths/role.

Indicators: EC-1, EC-2

Step 2

Research

4 foundation questions with 2 verified sources (NGOs, gov't, Wikipedia).

Indicators: EC-1, EC-3

Step 3

Log

14-day daily action journal: target area, materials, parent pitch, activity.

Indicators: EC-3, EC-4

Step 4

Measure

Quantitative impact tracking (e.g. kg diverted, kWh saved, hours served).

Indicators: EC-4, EC-5

Step 5

Reflect

Citizenship growth: what changed in me, what I will keep doing.

Indicators: EC-2, EC-5

5. Assessment Rubric

Each indicator is scored on a 3-point scale. The Qwen AI assistant (StatusPanel.html → LLM Quality Reviewer) drafts the level and feedback; the teacher reviews and publishes the final score.

Indicator Excellent (E) Adequate (A) Needs Work (N)
EC-1
Need Selection		 Clearly justifies a specific, well-evidenced local/global need; connects issue to systemic root causes. Identifies a real need with some justification; cause-and-effect is partial or surface-level. Need is vague, generic, or copied; little to no evidence of why it matters.
EC-2
Personal Fit		 Honestly evaluates specific skills/strengths/interests and matches them to concrete project tasks. Lists strengths in general terms; weak link to actual project tasks. No reflection on personal fit, or generic/boilerplate response.
EC-3
Plan		 Plan is realistic, time-bound, and assigns clear roles; logistics and parent/community pitch are concrete. Plan covers the basics but is missing roles, timeline, or material list. Plan is incomplete, unrealistic, or absent.
EC-4
Implementation		 Action log shows 14 days of consistent, well-documented activity with adaptations to obstacles. Action log is present but uneven, with gaps or thin daily entries. Action log is missing, mostly empty, or shows no real execution.
EC-5
Citizenship Growth		 Reflection names specific citizenship skills developed and how the student will sustain them. Reflection names some growth but stays at "I learned to help" level. Reflection is absent, gibberish, or unrelated to citizenship learning.

Quality Signals the AI Reviewer Flags

  • Lapse Keyboard mashes or single-character repeats (e.g. "eeeeeeeeeeee").
  • Lapse Citations that are AI chatbots, social media, or placeholder text.
  • Lapse Empty headers (e.g. listing "-parent script:" with no content).
  • Watch Single weak source (e.g. Wikipedia only, no NGO or government report).
