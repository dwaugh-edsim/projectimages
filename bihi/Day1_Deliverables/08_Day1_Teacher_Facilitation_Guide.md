## 🎨 The Desk Name Tent Sign Activity (Once Per Student)
*Cardstock &bull; 15 Minutes &bull; Displayed Daily on Desks*

### 5 Requirements (Scored on Back Panel out of 10):
1. **First & Last Name (2 pts):** Large, bold, readable from 15+ feet away.
2. **Three Logos / Symbols (3 pts):** 3 icons representing personal hobbies, music, sports, or culture.
3. **Three Colors (3 pts):** At least 3 distinct colors used.
4. **Neatness (1 pt):** Clean folding and clear lettering.
5. **Design & Layout (1 pt):** Balanced composition and filled space.

### Self-Assessment Format (Students write on back panel):
```text
First & Last Name  ___ / 2        Neatness         ___ / 1
Three Logos        ___ / 3        Three Colors     ___ / 3
--------------------------        Design / Layout  ___ / 1       TOTAL: [  /10 ]
Content Subtotal   ___ / 5        Design Subtotal  ___ / 5
```
