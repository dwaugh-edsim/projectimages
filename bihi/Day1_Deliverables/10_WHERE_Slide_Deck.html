1. The Personal Place Wallace, Nova Scotia
📍
Wallace, NS
North Shore • Northumberland Strait
Reflection: This place is my family's cottage. My parents bought the land next to the ocean from a farmer a few years before I was born. When I was a kid we used to spend the entire summer up there, swimming, fishing, picking berries and playing with the other kids, most of whom were from Halifax like me. We're all still friends.
Personal Perspective: This is a very special place for me. My wife and I got married on the front porch of the cottage 25 years ago this month. Her family flew from Korea to attend the wedding. They loved the scenery, the fresh air, and the seals swimming around my dad's boat in the water. We come back every summer with our two children so they could be with their Canadian grandparents.

2. The Community Place Dartmouth Bridges
🌉
Dartmouth & Halifax
Macdonald & MacKay Bridges
Reflection: I'm a Dartmouth person, and all my life I've had intimate knowledge of the two bridges. Though the two bridges should be called the "big" bridge and the "small" bridge — the MacKay bridge is much wider than the Macdonald — everyone in the city calls the MacKay the "new" bridge and the other the "old". That's funny considering the "new" bridge is older (1969) than I am, and I'm old.
People and the Land: The bridges are the beating heart of the city. Every day, 100,000 vehicles cross the bridges, likely the same ones at least twice in morning and afternoon commutes. The bridges opened up development in Dartmouth and Cole Harbour after the 1950s.

3. The Global Place Gwangju, South Korea
🇰🇷 🌏
Gwangju, S. Korea
Jeollanam-do Province
Reflection: Over 20 years ago my wife and I left Canada, first to Gwangju, South Korea. We had our first daughter there in 2003, a year before we left Korea. We go back to Gwangju sometimes: it's a strange place because it's a big city of over two million people, but it feels much smaller.
Family Tie-in: This is my wife's family's region. Gwangju is especially famous for its kimchi, and there's even a giant Kimchi festival to celebrate the food. My mother-in-law is especially proud of her kimchi. Kimchi is eaten during breakfast, lunch, and dinner.
Secret: I don't like kimchi. Please don't tell anyone.

4. The Aspirational Place Mauritius, Indian Ocean
🏝️ 🇲🇺
Mauritius
Indian Ocean • East of Madagascar
Reflection: While studying at Saint Mary's I met someone from Mauritius. I had no idea where Mauritius was so I looked it up on a map. What an incredible location: out in the Indian Ocean off the coast of Africa. She described it like paradise: warm, tropical, friendly, safe — and French. 30 years later and I still haven't been... but I'll go someday.
Personal Reflection: I've always wanted to experience a place that feels like a true blend of cultures. Mauritius, with its mix of African, Indian, French, and Chinese influences, seems like the perfect place. It shows me that a peaceful and beautiful future can be built from many different stories.
