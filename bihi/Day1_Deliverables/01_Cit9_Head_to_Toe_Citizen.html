Activity: What does an active, engaged citizen think, see, and do in daily life? Map your ideas onto the anatomical categories below. This canvas will be kept in your class portfolio and returned in June during Class 90 to see how your thinking developed over the year.
HEAD / MIND
What critical questions does an informed citizen ask? What knowledge do they seek out?
EYES / SIGHT
What community needs, systemic issues, or unfairness do they notice?
EARS / LISTENING
Whose lived experiences and quieter perspectives do they make an effort to listen to?
MOUTH / VOICE
How do they use their voice to educate, advocate, or speak up for others?
"Every citizen is shaped by what they notice, believe, and choose to do."
HEART / EMPATHY
What core values and principles of fairness guide their actions?
BACKBONE / COURAGE
How do they demonstrate moral courage when it is difficult or unpopular?
HANDS / ACTION
What practical service, community projects, or collective work do they create?
FEET / SHOWING UP
Where do they stand, walk, or physically and digitally show up for their community?
