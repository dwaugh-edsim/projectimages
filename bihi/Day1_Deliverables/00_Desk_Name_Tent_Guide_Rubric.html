Desk Name Tent Sign & Self-Assessment

Room 8 • Mr. Waugh • Day 1 Icebreaker & Classroom Name Display

Activity Time: 15–20 min
Total: 10 Points

How to Make Your Desk Sign

  1. Fold your cardstock horizontally down the middle so it stands upright like a tent.
  2. On the front panel, write your First and Last Name in large, bold letters.
  3. Add your pronouns and any helpful pronunciation notes.
  4. Draw 3 personal logos/symbols representing your interests or identity.
  5. Use at least 3 distinct colors to make it vibrant and neat.
  6. On the back panel, write and calculate your Self-Assessment Score (out of 10).
✨ Helpful Details to Add to Your Card
1. Pronouns (Optional) e.g., she/her, he/him, they/them in the top-right corner.
2. Name Pronunciation Phonetic guide if helpful (e.g., "pronounced: rye-lee").
3. One-Word Superpower One strength you bring (e.g., Creative, Focused, Listener, Resilient).
