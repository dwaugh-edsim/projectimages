# Healthy Living 8 Curriculum Guide

## Outcomes, Main Themes, and Suggested Activities

---

### **Outcome 1: Learners will analyse how life skills influence physical, mental, emotional, social, and spiritual health**

#### **Main Themes (Concepts)**
- Healthy Decision-Making
- Healthy Ways of Coping
- Interpersonal Skills that Enhance Health

#### **Suggested Activities & Focus Areas (Indicators)**
- **Analyse** factors that impact healthy decision making
- **Investigate** healthy ways of coping with difficult emotions and challenging life circumstances
- **Investigate** interpersonal skills that enhance health

### **Outcome 2: Learners will analyse the relationships between health behaviours and physical, mental, emotional, social, and spiritual health**

#### **Main Themes (Concepts)**
- Food Choices and Eating Habits
- Relationship Between Sleep and Health
- Relationships between Physical Activity and Health
- Healthy Behaviours for Technology
- Communicable Disease Prevention

#### **Suggested Activities & Focus Areas (Indicators)**
- **Investigate** healthy behaviours for using new technologies and media
- **Question** the relationship between sleep and health
- **Investigate** the relationship between physical activity and health
- **Analyse** food choices and eating habits that contribute to health
- **Compare** ways for preventing the spread of communicable diseases

### **Outcome 3: Learners will reflect on the importance of mental health literacy**

#### **Main Themes (Concepts)**
- Function of the Brain
- Stress and Anxiety in Maintaining Positive Mental Health
- Mental Health Stigma
- Help Seeking

#### **Suggested Activities & Focus Areas (Indicators)**
- **Investigate** the function of the brain in health and illness
- **Investigate** the role of stress and anxiety in maintaining positive mental health
- **Evaluate** the impact of the myths and realities of mental health stigma
- **Analyse** help seeking strategies

#### **Worthwhile Resources for this Outcome**
- *Currently no pre-approved PDF resources specifically for mental health literacy in the HL8 resources. Interactive simulation "BEHIND THE FEED" or "BRAIN UNDER CONSTRUCTION" acts as the unit spine.*

### **Outcome 4: Learners will evaluate ways to access reliable sexual health information that positively impacts adolescent sexual and reproductive health**

#### **Main Themes (Concepts)**
- Methods of Preventing Unintended Pregnancy and Sexually Transmitted Infections
- Adolescent Growth and Development
- Body Image
- Sexual Violence Prevention

#### **Suggested Activities & Focus Areas (Indicators)**
- **Compare** methods of preventing unintended pregnancy and blood borne illnesses
- **Investigate** physical, emotional, mental, social, and spiritual changes associated with adolescent growth and development
- **Analyse** how body image impacts adolescent health
- **Investigate** strategies for reducing sexual violence

### **Outcome 5: Learners will reflect on how relationships impact physical, mental, emotional, social, and spiritual health**

#### **Main Themes (Concepts)**
- Healthy Ways of Addressing Challenges in Relationships
- Engaging in Safe Relationships
- Recognizing Healthy and Unhealthy Relationships
- Coercive Behaviours and Associated Impacts
- Respecting the Rights of Others

#### **Suggested Activities & Focus Areas (Indicators)**
- **Investigate** the importance of addressing challenges in relationships with family, friends, or peers
- **Formulate** ways for engaging in safe relationships online and offline
- **Analyse** ways of recognizing healthy and unhealthy relationships
- **Identify** how to respond safely to coercive behaviours
- **Reflect** on the importance of respecting the rights of others

### **Outcome 6: Learners will reflect on the impact of substance misuse on adolescent health**

#### **Main Themes (Concepts)**
- Confronting Social Influences and Pressures
- Coping with Pressures and Difficult Emotions
- Injury Related to Substance Misuse
- Harms Associated with the Use and Co-use

#### **Suggested Activities & Focus Areas (Indicators)**
- **Formulate** strategies for confronting social influences and pressures to use substances
- **Compare** strategies for coping with pressures and difficult emotions
- **Investigate** injuries related to substance misuse
- **Investigate** the harms associated with the use and co-use of alcohol, cannabis, tobacco, vaping products, caffeine, and prescription drugs
