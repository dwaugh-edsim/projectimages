# Healthy Living 8 — Simulation / Activity Concepts (for selection)

**Purpose:** 2–3 concept options per unit (outcome). Mark your choice per unit with `[x]`, amend notes inline. Selected concepts get built to the exhibit-by-exhibit level (like `leo_1752`) and slotted into `outline.md` as multi-session blocks.

**Shared architecture (all options):** narrative/scenario hook → numbered "exhibits" with analysis tabs → student **choices + written rationales** → `code.gs` Apps Script → Google Sheet → teacher dashboard → culminating decision/verdict. New sims are just new `missionId`s + a re-skinned HTML/exhibit template.

**Legend per concept:**
- **Hook** — the scenario that pulls students in
- **Exhibits** — the numbered scenario parts students work through (≈1 session each)
- **Choice + rationale** — what students decide and justify (logged to Sheets)
- **Culmination** — the closing decision/verdict
- **Build** — rough effort (Light / Medium / Heavy)
- **Resources** — which `resources/` PDFs it draws on · **GAP FILLER** = unit has little/no material, sim carries the unit

## Unit 1 — Outcome 1: Healthy Decision-Making & Coping
*Sub-themes: Healthy Decision-Making · Healthy Coping · Interpersonal Skills · Resource: W812 Responsibilities & Consequences*

### [ ] A — "JUNCTION" (branching narrative sim)
- **Hook:** You're "Sam," grade 8. One evening: a group chat is blowing up, your project partner bailed, and a parent is stressed — three fires at once.
- **Exhibits:** 1) the group-chat dilemma 2) the school-stress tipping point 3) a peer confiding something heavy. Tabs each: Situation / Coping Toolkit / Consequences.
- **Choice + rationale:** pick a coping skill + decision per exhibit; sim reveals the ripple.
- **Culmination:** "Life-Skill Defense" — defend your decision chain.
- **Build:** Medium · **Resources:** W812

### [ ] B — "CONSEQUENCE CALCULATOR" (decision-tree game)
- **Hook:** Build and trace decision trees for grade-8 scenarios; see weighted consequences stack up.
- **Exhibits:** 1) shared sample tree 2) student-authored scenario 3) peer-plays-each-other's-trees.
- **Choice + rationale:** at each node choose a path + justify; weighted outcomes reveal short vs long-term impact.
- **Culmination:** "Best-Path" analysis of a peer's tree.
- **Build:** Medium · **Resources:** W812 + student-authored

### [ ] C — "COPING TOOLKIT LAB" (skills-builder)
- **Hook:** Assemble a personal coping toolkit, then stress-test it against generated "difficult emotion" scenarios.
- **Exhibits:** 1) emotion-sorter 2) strategy lab (match strategy→emotion→context) 3) interpersonal-skills role cards.
- **Choice + rationale:** select + justify coping strategies; system rates fit.
- **Culmination:** Personalized "Toolkit Card" export.
- **Build:** Light · **Resources:** W812 (supplemented)

## Day 1 / Planning Adjustments (do this before finalizing `outline.md`)

**Prior-course Google Classroom audit — BIG priority, Day 1.**
At a new school, before locking the plan, get **one student to share their screen** showing what they actually did last year:
- **Grade 8 students → their Healthy Living 7** Google Classroom (what the last teacher covered, how deep, what they skipped)
- **Grade 9 students → their Healthy Living 8** Google Classroom (same)

**Why it matters:** this reveals prior knowledge, gaps, what's already been over-taught, and the previous teacher's style — all of which shift emphasis and sequencing in `outline.md`. Easier to modify the scaffold than to build from a cold start.

**What to capture from the audit (note in `outline.md` later):**
- [ ] Which outcomes/sub-themes were already covered deeply → trim or skip ahead
- [ ] Which were skipped or shallow → give more sessions
- [ ] Any activities/topics students are sick of → swap concept options
- [ ] Prior teacher's assessment style → calibrate quiz/test expectations
- [ ] Tech fluency (did they use simulations/Sheets?) → calibrate how much onboarding a `leo`-style sim needs

**Holds:** Don't finalize `outline.md` session counts or sim picks until after this audit. The concept options above are intentionally broad enough to bend to whatever the audit reveals.
