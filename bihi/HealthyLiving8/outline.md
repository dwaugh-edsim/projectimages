---

## 1. Course Overview

Healthy Living 8 delivers Nova Scotia's Grade 8 Health Education curriculum across **~90 classes** running from September to mid-June, on a **5-classes-per-2-week-cycle schedule (~2.5 per week)**. The course covers all 6 curriculum outcomes through a mix of direct instruction, scenario-based learning, discussion, structured role-plays, teacher-built simulations (where they exist), project work, and reflection. Every outcome is taught with **14–16 classes on average** — giving students deep exposure — and each unit ends in a dedicated review class followed by a summative quiz or project milestone.

The course features active integration of student simulations to anchor key learning outcomes. For outcomes with scarce resources, the simulation is the primary learning vehicle (e.g., Mental Health and Substance Misuse).

---

## 2. Sequencing Rationale

Outcomes are ordered to build skills from foundational to sensitive, with deliberate pacing decisions:

1. **Foundations of Decision-Making & Health Behaviours (O1 → O2):** Students establish what health is, how to make decisions, and how sleep, tech, exercise, disease prevention, and nutrition impact their lives.
2. **Mental Health Literacy (O3):** Positioned in late Fall, giving students concrete coping and help-seeking strategies before winter.
3. **Sexual and Reproductive Health (O4):** Taught in winter. Uses ground rules and anonymous questions to manage sensitive topics.
4. **Healthy Relationships & Violence Prevention (O5):** Translates communication and boundary skills into interpersonal safety (online and offline).
5. **Substance Misuse Prevention (O6):** Positioned in spring, addressing peer pressure and harm reduction in preparation for summer.
6. **Capstone Portfolio:** Curates and reflects on learning at the end of the year.

## 4. Term 1 — Sept to January (Classes 1–45)

### OUTCOME 1 — Life Skills and Health (Classes 1–12, 12 classes)
*Analyse how life skills influence physical, mental, emotional, social, and spiritual health.*
*Sub-themes: Healthy Decision-Making · Healthy Ways of Coping · Interpersonal Skills that Enhance Health*
*Vocabulary in play: life skills, decision-making, coping, interpersonal skills, refusal skills, active listening, assertiveness, negotiation, peer pressure, stressor, 5 dimensions of health*

**Simulation pick: Option A — "JUNCTION"** (branching narrative sim).
*Students assume the role of "Sam," navigating group chats, project partner issues, and family stress.*
*Span: Classes 3, 5, 8, 10. Culmination: "Life-Skill Defense" in Class 11.*

---

**Class 1 — "Course Kickoff: The 5 Dimensions of Health" (Cycle 1)**
- **Activity:** Introduction to the 5 dimensions of health (physical, mental, emotional, social, spiritual). Students complete a self-reflection map of their own life dimensions.
- **Materials:** Dimensions of Health diagram, student self-mapping worksheet.
- **Homework/Extension:** None.
- **Assessment:** Exit ticket: Define "life skills" and name one challenge in your life that requires a life skill.

**Class 2 — "Understanding Stressors & Coping" (Cycle 1)**
- **Activity:** Group brainstorm on common Grade 8 stressors. Introduce healthy vs. unhealthy coping strategies (avoidance vs. problem-solving).
- **Materials:** Stressor categorization chart.
- **Homework/Extension:** Journal Entry 1: Write down 3 stressors you encountered this week and how you coped.
- **Assessment:** Formative check on identifying healthy vs. unhealthy coping mechanisms.

**Class 3 — "Junction Sim Launch: Sam's World" (Cycle 1)**
- **Activity:** Introduce "Junction" branching narrative sim. Class walks through Exhibit 1 (Group-chat dilemma). Students make individual choices and document rationales.
- **Materials:** Junction Sim access / Exhibit 1 sheets.
- **Homework/Extension:** None.
- **Assessment:** Choice submission in Junction Sim.

**Class 4 — "Decision-Making Steps" (Cycle 1)**
- **Activity:** Walkthrough of the 6-step decision-making model. Apply it to hypothetical scenarios in small groups.
- **Materials:** Decision-making model handout.
- **Homework/Extension:** Review decision-making steps.
- **Assessment:** Group presentation of scenario solutions.

**Class 5 — "Junction Exhibit 2: Tipping Point" (Cycle 1)**
- **Activity:** Junction Sim Exhibit 2 (School-stress tipping point). Focus on coping mechanisms under academic pressure.
- **Materials:** Junction Sim access / Exhibit 2 sheets.
- **Homework/Extension:** Reflect on a time you made a decision under stress.
- **Assessment:** Junction Sim response sheet.

**Class 6 — "Interpersonal Skills: Respecting Boundaries" (Cycle 2)**
- **Activity:** Define boundaries and explore assertiveness vs. passivity vs. aggression in personal relationships.
- **Materials:** Boundaries worksheet, role-play scenario cards.
- **Homework/Extension:** Complete boundary-setting worksheet.
- **Assessment:** Classroom role-play observation.

**Class 7 — "Active Listening & Negotiation" (Cycle 2)**
- **Activity:** Partner exercise on active listening (paraphrasing, clarifying questions). Introduce basic negotiation tactics for conflict resolution.
- **Materials:** Active listening scorecard.
- **Homework/Extension:** None.
- **Assessment:** Partner feedback checklist.

**Class 8 — "Junction Exhibit 3: The Heavy Secret" (Cycle 2)**
- **Activity:** Junction Sim Exhibit 3 (Peer confides something heavy). Focus on the ethics of secrets, safety, and when to get a trusted adult.
- **Materials:** Junction Sim access / Exhibit 3 sheets.
- **Homework/Extension:** Journal Entry 2: When is it right to break a friend's trust for their safety?
- **Assessment:** Junction Sim response.

**Class 9 — "Refusal Skills & Peer Pressure" (Cycle 2)**
- **Activity:** Practising the 4 refusal styles (direct, excuse, delay, alternative). Socratic seminar on indirect vs. direct peer pressure.
- **Materials:** Refusal skills reference sheet.
- **Homework/Extension:** Think of a refusal scenario to role-play in Class 10.
- **Assessment:** Formative peer check during refusal practice.

**Class 10 — "Junction Sim Wrap-up & Class Synthesis" (Cycle 2)**
- **Activity:** Junction Sim culminates with a reflection on how decision chains affect the 5 dimensions of health.
- **Materials:** Junction Sim compilation sheet.
- **Homework/Extension:** Prep for Junction Defense.
- **Assessment:** Completion of Junction Sim database response.

**Class 11 — "Life-Skill Defense & Unit Review" (Cycle 3)**
- **Activity:** Students present their "Life-Skill Defense" rationales in groups of three. Whole-class jeopardy game covering key terms and models.
- **Materials:** Jeopardy board, Unit 1 study guide.
- **Homework/Extension:** Study for Unit 1 test.
- **Assessment:** Formative review performance.

**Class 12 — 📋 SUMMATIVE: Life Skills & Health Test (Cycle 3)**
- **Activity:** Summative test (MC, matching, scenario-based short answer).
- **Materials:** Unit 1 Test sheets.
- **Homework/Extension:** None.
- **Assessment:** Unit 1 test score (**15% of final grade**).
