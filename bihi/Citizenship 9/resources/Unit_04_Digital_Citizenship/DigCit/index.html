    📋 Field Guide: How to Spot Deepfakes

    • Weird hands/fingers: AI often generates extra fingers or strange hand positions
    • Asymmetric features: Mismatched earrings, different eye shapes, uneven smile
    • Gibberish text: Signs, labels, or backgrounds with nonsense characters
    • Strange reflections: Mirrors, glasses, or shiny surfaces that don't match reality
    • Blurry or smudged details: Especially around hair, jewelry, or complex patterns
    • Impossible physics: Objects that defy gravity or overlap unnaturally
