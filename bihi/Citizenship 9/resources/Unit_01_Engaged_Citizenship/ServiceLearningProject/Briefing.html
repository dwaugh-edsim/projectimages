Objective 02: Resource Validation

Fact-Checking

Command requires verified intel. Avoid surface-level summaries. We need data, laws, and specific global impacts.

  • Find at least TWO reliable sources (Government reports, NGOs, Wikipedia)
  • WHAT IS AN NGO?
    NGO (Non-Governmental Organization): A non-profit group that operates independently of any government. They are often the best sources for environmental and social justice data (e.g. Greenpeace, EAC, etc.). Wikipedia is also a great starting point for finding these groups!
  • Cite your sources at the end of each response
  • Map the 'Supply Chain' or 'Systemic Barrier' if required by your mission
