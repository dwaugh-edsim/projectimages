# 🗳️ The Campaign Trail: Election Assignment Guide

*Since 5 parties have successfully locked in their platforms, it is time to move to the campaign phase. This guide outlines exactly what students must produce and how "Election Day" will run.*

---

## 1. First Stage Deliverables (What they do NOW)
Welcome to the Official Halifax West Election Campaign! Since the platforms (Comms, Strategy, Finance) are already complete and posted to the Official Voter Guide, groups must immediately split up and work on the following three deliverables:

1.  **The 2-Minute Launch Speech (Use Google Docs):** A written script designed to be spoken out loud. It must merge their Communications Message, 3 Policy Points, and end with their Official Slogan. One group member will write this on a shared Google Doc.
2.  **The Campaign Slide Deck (Use Canva or Google Slides):** A highly visual, 3-4 slide presentation that will play on the projector behind the speaker. 
    *   *Slide 1:* Party Name, Leader, Slogan, and Colors.
    *   *Slide 2:* The Core Platform (The 3 main promises).
    *   *Slide 3:* The Budget (Where the money is coming from).
    *   *Action:* Have your Campaign Manager launch Canva or Google Slides to build this deck.
3.  **The Opposition Research (Use the provided Web App):** Using the digital *Halifax West Opposition Research Hub*, students must read the platforms of their rivals and draft **one highly specific, respectful attack question** targeting a flaw in a rival's plan.

---

## 2. Group Roles (How 2-3 people divide the work)
To ensure no student is sitting idle, the deliverables and stage presentation are strictly divided:

### 👤 The Campaign Manager (Visuals & Data)
*   **Prep Duty:** Creates the Campaign Slide Deck (PPT). Ensures the slides are visual, professional, and clearly show *how* the party will pay for things.
*   **Stage Duty:** Operates the slides during the speech.

### 🎤 The Party Leader (The Pitch)
*   **Prep Duty:** Writes and practices the 2-Minute Launch Speech. Works on tone, pacing, and hitting the "vibe" of the party.
*   **Stage Duty:** Stands at the front and delivers the 120-second speech to the class.

### 🛡️ The Policy Strategist (The Opposition)
*   **Prep Duty:** Logs into the *Opposition Research Web App*. Reads rival platforms and writes the "Attack Questions."
*   **Stage Duty:** When the floor opens for Opposition, they are the one who stands up to grill the rival parties. They also jump in to defend their own party if their budget or policies are attacked.

*(Note: For groups of 2, the Leader delivers the speech, and the Deputy acts as the combined Manager & Strategist).*

---

## 3. The Process (Two-Round Election Format)
With 8-10 political parties, running a full debate for everyone takes too long. Instead, we use a "Primary" and a "Final Four" showdown.

### Round 1: The Primary Pitches
1.  **The Pitch (2 mins per party):** Every single party (all 8-10) takes the stage. The Party Manager runs the Canva/Slides, and the Leader delivers their 2-minute Launch Speech. *No questions are asked during this round.*
2.  **The Primary Vote:** The class holds a quick "Primary Vote" via Google Form. The top 4 parties with the most votes advance to the General Election.

### Round 2: The Final Four Town Hall
1.  **The Stage:** The Leaders of the "Final 4" parties bring chairs to the front of the room. 
2.  **The Press Gallery:** The students from the eliminated parties (4-6 groups) now become the "Press Gallery." They open the *Opposition Research Hub* and use the "Prep Pad" to aggressively target flaws in the Final 4's platforms.
3.  **The Grilling (15-20 mins):** You (the moderator) call on the Press Gallery. They fire their tough questions at the Final 4 Leaders. The leaders must respond and defend their policies live on stage.

### Round 3: The General Election
After the Town Hall, the entire class casts a final secret ballot to choose the ultimate winner from the Final 4. 
*   **Crucial Rule:** Students *cannot* vote for their own party. They must vote based on who survived the Town Hall pressure!
