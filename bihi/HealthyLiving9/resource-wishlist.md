## 4. HL8 Resources That Carry Over

The teacher had approximately 20 PDFs in the Grade 8 resources folder. Based on the HL8 curriculum topics and HL9 extensions (documented in hl9-outcomes-enhanced.md), the following HL8 materials likely remain relevant:

### ✅ Definitely Carry Over (reuse in HL9)

| HL8 Resource (estimated) | Used in HL9 Outcome(s) | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Comprehensive Sexual Health Education framework/guide** | Outcome 2 | HL9 builds on HL8 sexual health foundation — contraception, decision-making are new layers on existing anatomy/puberty content |
| **Consent framework/reference** | Outcomes 2, 4, 5, 6, 7 | Consent concepts carry into every relationship and safety unit. HL8 consent materials still accurate |
| **Healthy vs. Unhealthy Relationships guide** | Outcomes 4, 5, 6 | HL9 extends to communication skills and power dynamics, but the baseline healthy/unhealthy framework is the same |
| **Mental Health Literacy basics** (brain, stress, stigma) | Outcomes 9, 10, 11 | HL8's mental health foundation feeds directly into HL9's resilience, coping, and help-seeking units |
| **Bullying Prevention resources** | Outcomes 4, 7, 8 | HL9 extends to bystander intervention and GBV, but bullying basics remain relevant |
| **Substance Misuse introductory materials** | Outcome 10 | HL9 goes much deeper into addiction, but HL8's pressure/harm basics are the starting point |
| **Cyberbullying / Online Safety basics** | Outcomes 4, 7 | HL9 adds grooming, sextortion, digital rights — but cyberbullying prevention content is still used |
| **Conflict Resolution introduction** | Outcome 4 | HL9 needs much deeper assertiveness/negotiation/refusal skills, but the basic conflict resolution framework from HL8 applies |
| **Stress Management / Coping basics** | Outcomes 9, 11 | HL9 extends to protective factors and rest/fun/relaxation practices, but coping strategy foundations carry over |

### ⚡ Partially Carry Over (needs supplementation)

| HL8 Resource (estimated) | Used in HL9 Outcome(s) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Puberty / Reproductive Anatomy materials** | Outcome 2 | Reference material only — HL9 focuses on contraception, decision-making, and treatment access, not anatomy review |
| **Decision-Making model** (general) | Outcomes 2, 10 | The framework carries over but needs to be applied to new, more complex scenarios |
| **Dimensions of Health framework** | Outcomes 1, 9, 10 | Still the organizing framework, but applied to new content |

### ❌ Do NOT Carry Over (HL9-specific new content)

- Gender norms, stereotypes, biases — **entirely new in HL9** (Outcome 5)
- Gender-based violence — **entirely new in HL9** (Outcome 6)
- Health literacy & healthcare navigation — **entirely new in HL9** (Outcome 3)
- Bystander intervention as a dedicated unit — **new in HL9** (Outcome 8, though some HL8 bystander concepts exist)
- Online victimization beyond cyberbullying — **new in HL9** (grooming, sextortion in Outcome 7)
- Help-seeking efficacy as a dedicated unit — **new in HL9** (Outcome 11, though HL8 touched on it)

### Recommended First Actions

1. **Audit HL8 PDFs** — Before downloading anything, review the ~20 HL8 PDFs and tag each one: KEEP (for HL9), PARTIAL (needs supplementation), or REPLACE (HL9 needs different content).
2. **Download HIGH-priority resources first** — Units 5, 6, 8, 10 have NO HL8 carry-over. These must be built from scratch.
3. **Bookmark all websites** — Many resources are web-based and may not have downloadable PDFs. Create an organized bookmark folder structure matching the 11 outcomes.
4. **Check the NS EECD curriculum support site** — There may be official supplementary documents for Healthy Living 9 that aren't the AAG (curriculum guide). Check for learning activities, assessment exemplars, and resource lists.
5. **Check for W-series documents** — The W-series (e.g., W93) may contain learning activity resources specifically designed for the Grade 9 Healthy Living outcomes.
