Angels and Demons




A large oceanfront cottage one hour from downtown Halifax is going to be the site of an anti-grad party the Sunday after prom.


About a hundred people are planning on coming, and most have prepared stories for parents. There are more people going than there are seats in cars, so many cars will be overloaded, and it’s known that a few cars will be taking teens in the trunk for the hour-long trip. 


The property has a wharf and shed with two kayaks, two canoes, two jet skis and an oil drum full of inflatable tubes and water toys. There’s also a plastic crate full of fireworks. 


Everyone is supposed to bring sleeping bags, and a bunch of tents are being rounded up. No one has organized food. Everyone is just bringing whatever they might want to cook on bbqs or snack on through the night. The two hosts have a new drinking game planned that they found on youtube. 


There’s already talk about who wants to hook up with who before the summer starts and people go their separate ways after high school. 


There’s a swimming pool and a seven-person Jacuzzi, with a large trampoline a few metres away. The shoreline has a wharf, but the closest beach is a sandy cove a five-minute boat ride away


A local DJ named FEEDING FRENZY who works weekends spinning at illegal after-hours parties has agreed to work the night for one of the guys who is graduating and co-hosting the event. The DJ gets to bring friends and use the cottage for a couple days after, as well as payment in steaks, beer, vodka and weed. 


One of the friends tagging along will be Goldilocks – a popular drug dealer in Halifax. He’ll have an assortment of drugs to sell or give away for free - depending on his mood and motives. Another friend of the DJ is bringing a high def video camera and is hoping to get some of the same kind of sketchy footage he taped during an off-campus party last winter.
