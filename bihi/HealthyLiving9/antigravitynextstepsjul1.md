## Priority 1 — Fill the three empty folders

### Theme_03_Accessing_Health_Resources (Outcome 3)

The Grade 9 curriculum asks students to find reliable health info and navigate the healthcare system. We need: how to book a doctor in NS, walk-in clinics, telehealth, IWK Youth Health, 211, Kids Help Phone, school-based supports.

**Targets:**
- **211 Nova Scotia service directory** — `https://211.ca/find-help` (or the NS service page). This is the front door for "where do I go for X in Nova Scotia." Have kids build a personal resource card.
- **IWK Youth Health resources** — `https://www.iwk.nshealth.ca/youth` (VERIFY current URL). The IWK is the NS children's/women's hospital and is the canonical source for youth health navigation in NS.
- **Nova Scotia Health — Your Health NS** — `https://www.nshealth.ca/your-health` or the patient-and-family-resources section. Walk-in clinic finder, 811 nurse line, Mental Health and Addictions intake.
- **Health811 (formerly 811 Telehealth)** — `https://811.novascotia.ca` (VERIFY — service was rebranded from 811 in 2023). Phone line staffed 24/7 by RNs. Critical NS-specific resource.
- **Kids Help Phone — Resources Around Me** — `https://kidshelpphone.ca/get-help/resources-around-me/`. Free, confidential, 24/7. Should be in every Grade 9 student's phone.
- **Teen Health Source (Planned Parenthood NS)** — `https://teenhealthsource.com` (VERIFY). Sexual health info specifically for teens, NS-focused.
- **Canadian Paediatric Society — youth health literacy page** — `https://caringforkids.cps.ca/handouts/your_childs_health`. Trusted by NS paediatricians.
- **Government of Canada — Health services in your province** — `https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/health-care-system/provincial-territorial-health-services.html` for NS-specific.

Save each as a PDF via print-to-PDF in a browser, or use any direct PDF download links you can find on the same sites. Filename convention: `OrgName_ResourceTitle.pdf`.

## Quality expectations (READ BEFORE DOWNLOADING)

1. **Files must be real PDFs/docx, not placeholder text files.**
2. **Use descriptive filenames** following the existing convention. Examples: `GreenDot_HighSchoolBystanderGuide.pdf`, `IWK_YouthHealthNavigation.pdf`, `PREVNet_RelationshipCommunication.pdf`. Not `doc1.pdf` or `download.pdf`.
3. **Skip paywalled content** unless a free sample/preview is available. If a full curriculum is paid, save whatever free preview/sample materials exist.
4. **Skip content for adults only** or for significantly different age groups (no elementary, no adult-only, no college-only).
5. **For copyrighted teacher resources** (Fourth R, Green Dot full curriculum), check if a free intro/sample is downloadable. Don't break copyright. Add a README.md in the folder if you had to fall back to sample materials.
6. **Avoid making more than 3 files per folder** for already-populated folders. Aim for 2-5 files per folder total.
7. **When in doubt about a URL**, visit it first. If the page is dead, find an alternative. If you can't find an alternative, mark VERIFY in a `_notes.md` in that folder and move on.
