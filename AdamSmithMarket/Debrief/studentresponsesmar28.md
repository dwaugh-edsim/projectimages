## Common Errors to Watch For

### Term Confusion
- **Scarcity vs Shortage:** Scarcity is permanent/fundamental; shortage is temporary
- **Inflation vs Deflation:** Inflation = prices rise; Deflation = prices fall
- **Specialization vs Division of Labor:** Specialization = focus on one thing; Division of Labor = splitting tasks among workers
- **Barter vs Medium of Exchange:** Barter = direct trade; Medium of Exchange = using money/coins
