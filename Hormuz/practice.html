TRAINING PROTOCOLS

STAGE 1: THE Y-AXIS (PRICE)

The vertical axis represents Price ($). As you move UP, things get more expensive.

STAGE 2: THE X-AXIS (QUANTITY)

The horizontal axis represents Quantity (Q). As you move RIGHT, there is more stuff being bought/sold.

STAGE 3: BUYER INTUITION

Scenario: Gasoline prices just jumped to $3.00/L. What happens to the amount consumers want to buy?

STAGE 4: DRAWING DEMAND

Connect your logic. Draw the Demand curve showing that as price goes UP, quantity goes DOWN.

STAGE 5: SELLER INTUITION

Scenario: You own a gas station. If the price goes UP, do you want to sell more or less relative to the cheaper price?

STAGE 6: DRAWING SUPPLY

Draw the Supply curve showing that businesses provide more at higher prices.

STAGE 7: INELASTICITY

Gasoline is a "Must-Have." Draw a STEEP Demand curve to represent that behavior.
