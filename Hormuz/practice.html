STAGE 0: SECURITY CLEARANCE

Before accessing the terminal, HQ needs to verify your economic intuition. Pass the Socratic Check to unlock the simulators.

STAGE 1: AXIS IDENTIFICATION (PRICE)

Economics is built on two numbers. Identify the Price ($) axis. As it goes UP, the price increases.

  • Click the PRICE ($) label on the vertical axis.

STAGE 2: AXIS IDENTIFICATION (QUANTITY)

The horizontal axis represents Quantity (Q). This is how many units are being traded.

  • Click the QUANTITY (Q) label on the horizontal axis.

STAGE 3: THE Y-VALUE (PRICE)

Advisor, plot a test point at $3.00. High prices restrict the market.

  • Click exactly on the $3.00 mark on the Y-Axis.

STAGE 4: BUYER INTUITION

Market Equilibrium: The white dot shows the current balance. Scenario: Gas prices jumped to $3.00. Adjust the dot to $3.00 and 50 units to show how Buyers respond.

STAGE 5: DRAWING DEMAND

Connect your logic. Draw the Demand curve showing that as price goes UP, quantity goes DOWN.

STAGE 6: SELLER INTUITION

Market Equilibrium: Now consider the Sellers. If the price goes UP to $3.00, gas stations want more profit. Move the dot to $3.00 and 250 units.

STAGE 7: DRAWING SUPPLY

Draw the Supply curve showing that businesses provide more at higher prices.

STAGE 8: INELASTICITY (MUST-HAVES)

Some goods, like Gasoline, don't have easy substitutes. Even if price triples, people still HAVE to drive. This is Inelastic Demand.

  • Draw a STEEP, NEAR-VERTICAL line to show that quantity barely changes even at high prices.
