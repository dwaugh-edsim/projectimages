Market Forces

Understanding Demand & Supply as Social Behavior

  • Buyers: Respond to Price. Higher Price = Lower Quantity Demanded.
  • Sellers: Respond to Profit. Higher Price = Higher Quantity Supplied.
  • Equilibrium: The point where Buyer and Seller naturally agree.

The Supply Shock

What happens during a blockade?

  • Leftward Shift: Less gas is physically available globally.
  • Competition: Buyers fight over the remaining supply.
  • Scarcity: This drives the price UP automatically.

The Inelasticity Trap

Why oil is dangerous for an economy

  • No Substitutes: People HAVE to drive to work.
  • Vertical Demand: People pay almost any price to keep moving.
  • Economic Bleed: High gas prices suck money out of other sectors.
