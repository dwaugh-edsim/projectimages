WHAT IS TRADE?

 Trade is the action of buying and selling
  goods and services.

Example: Joan grew red peppers and sold
  them at the market to Jane for a
  monetary exchange. (don't copy)

 Most trade is no longer conducted by
  countries, but by multinational
  corporations (MNLs).
TWO MAIN TYPES OF TRADE

Free Trade

Fair Trade

FREE TRADE

Is a form of trade where there are
  agreements in place, and tariffs are
  lowered. Governments do not
  discriminate against imports
  allowing for global markets like
  today.

Product prices are susceptible to
  market fluctuations.

Unfortunately, the bigger the
  company (MNLs), the better the
  trade deals.
FREE TRADE ADVANTAGES:

Allows for variety in
 products.

Innovation : ability to create
 new products, ex. GMOs.

International cooperation.
Creates employment and

 economic growth.
FREE TRADE DISADVANTAGES:

Labour laws may not exist or not be
  enforced resulting in forced
  labour/child labour, low wages and
  long work hours.

Product safety; recalls.
FREE TRADE DISADVANTAGES:

Environmental laws may not
  exist or be enforced resulting in
  environmental catastrophes.

Multinational Corporations;
  outsourcing business (we have

   experienced this first hand in N.S.)
WHAT IS A FREE TRADE
AGREEMENT?

A pact between two or more
  countries or areas in which they
  agree to lift most or all tariffs,
  quotas, special fees and taxes,
  and other barriers to trade
  between them, NAFTA ­ North
  American Free Trade Agreement

FAIR TRADE ADVANTAGES

(refer to the 8 principles of fair trade)
 Money goes to the producers (the workers)
 Fair payment/living wage for the products
 No forced labour--People are not enslaved
 Child labour is against regulations
 Money goes back into the community to

  support health services, education and
  infrastructure.
 Programs deliberately designed to improve
  environmental sustainability
FAIR TRADE ADVANTAGES CONT'D.:

Prices remain constant to cover
  cost of production and cost of
  living.

Employees have access to credit
  to create cooperatives.

Long-term stable contracts.
FAIR TRADE DISADVANTAGES:

Certification process may
 cause companies to lose
 interest.

Tariffs may be higher.
Products may not be

 available everywhere.
