02

Select Choice Architecture Mechanisms (Up to 3)

Select up to 3 behavioral economics techniques you will use to shift consumer preferences and overcome the price barrier.

Anchoring

Placing the product next to a premium item to make it feel affordable.

e.g., Placing the $7.50 bar next to a $14.50 single-origin micro-batch bar.

Default Bias

Auto-selecting carbon offset/ethical options (requiring action to opt out).

e.g., Pre-checking "Upgrade this bar to Fair Trade cocoa (+ $1.50)" at checkout.

Social Proof

Showing that other people choose sustainable (e.g., "85% of your classmates choose sustainable").

e.g., Adding a sign: "82% of Halifax shoppers choose slave-free cocoa."

Ethical Priming

Point-of-purchase displays highlighting worker conditions to trigger moral decisions.

e.g., Displaying farmer cooperative profiles directly on the front packaging.

Decoy Effect

Creating a third, less-attractive option to guide choices.

e.g., Offering a slightly smaller standard bar for $6.80, making the large Fair Trade bar at $7.50 look like a steal.
03

Highlight Fair Trade Advantages (Select at least 3)

No Forced or Child Labor

Strict agricultural inspections to ban modern slavery. (e.g., Unannounced third-party farm audits to verify school attendance.)

Living Wage & Fair Payment

Paying prices that cover basic food, housing, and healthcare. (e.g., Establishing a guaranteed baseline above the volatile market price.)

Community Infrastructure

Revenues fund local public improvements. (e.g., Using social premiums to drill clean water wells or construct medical centers.)

Environmental Sustainability

Mandating resource conservation and chemical bans. (e.g., Replacing toxic chemical sprays with organic composting.)

Stable, Direct Contracts

Eliminating middlemen to secure direct partner pricing. (e.g., Signing multi-year buying agreements so cooperatives can obtain credit.)

Democratic Decision-Making

Producers vote on how to spend revenues. (e.g., Cooperative members vote collectively on whether to buy tractors or hire nurses.)

Safe Working Conditions

Enforcing occupational health and safety codes. (e.g., Providing protective gear, fire exits, and safe ventilation in sewing rooms.)

Gender Equality & Empowerment

Ensuring equal pay and representation. (e.g., Reserving leadership seats for female farmers and offering women's micro-loans.)
