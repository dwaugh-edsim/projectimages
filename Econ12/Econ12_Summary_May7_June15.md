## 🏛️ Week 1: Finishing the Market Spectrum
**Theme:** Moving from the "Broken" markets to the "Competitive Ideal."
- **[DONE] Monopoly & Oligopoly** (Concentrated market power)
- **[DONE] Market Failures** (Externalities and inefficiency)
- **[NEW] The Pit Market Simulation:** Classroom card game to experience supply/demand discovery.
- **Why Competition Matters:** Analysis of consumer surplus and efficiency.

## 🌍 Week 2: The Global Bridge (Trade)
**Theme:** The ethics of globalization and supply chains.
- **[FEATURED] Made in Bangladesh (CBC):** Documentary on the human cost of the garment industry.
- **A Game of No Rules:** Hands-on simulation exploring unregulated international trade.
- **Free Trade vs. Fair Trade:** Debate-based look at global economic policies.
- **Globalization (Crash Course):** High-speed overview of modern interconnectedness.

## 💳 Week 3: Wealth, Poverty & Personal Finance
**Theme:** How the economy affects the individual.
- **Spent (The Game):** Interactive online simulation of living on the poverty line.
- **The Game of Life:** Financial simulation covering budgeting and unexpected costs.
- **Income Inequality:** Looking at the wealth gap and potential solutions (UBI).
- **Credit Card Scenarios:** Practical exercises on debt and financial literacy.

## 📈 Week 4: The Big Picture (Future & Inflation)
**Theme:** Price stability and long-term sustainability.
- **John Oliver on Inflation:** Deep dive into why prices are rising (and who is to blame).
- **Inflation Bingo:** Interactive review of monetary concepts.
- **Sustainable Development:** Balancing growth with environmental limits.
- **The Carbon Tax:** Analysis of market-based tools for climate change.
