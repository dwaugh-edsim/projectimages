🏢 The LOSTCO Procurement Challenge

Scenario: You are a Procurement Specialist on the Ethical Sourcing Committee at **LOSTCO** (a national warehouse club known for selling items in bulk at low prices). Unlike boutique retailers, LOSTCO shoppers are extreme deal-hunters. To successfully stock a more expensive Fair Trade product, your committee must present a compelling proposal to the Board of Directors.

You must select two completed products from the classroom gallery, calculate the bulk cost premiums using the wholesale calculator, and draft your strategies to override the deal-conscious bulk buyer's mindset using choice architecture.
