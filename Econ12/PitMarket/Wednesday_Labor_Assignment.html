Task 1: The Dhaka Research Challenge

Use your Numbeo skills to compare Halifax, NS to Dhaka, Bangladesh (the heart of the global textile industry).

  • Rent in Dhaka is __________% lower than in Halifax.
  • Groceries in Dhaka are __________% lower than in Halifax.
  • Question: How does this cost difference explain why labor is a "cheaper commodity" in Bangladesh?
Task 2: The Logic of the Box

If the cost of living is 90% lower in Dhaka, and a shipping container makes it "costless" to move a T-shirt to Halifax, why would a company like Walmart ever choose to pay a Canadian worker to make that shirt?

Task 3: Are YOU a Commodity?

In a perfectly competitive global market, labor is treated like a commodity (identical product). How can a worker in Canada "exit" this competition and become a "Price Maker" instead of a "Price Taker"? (Hint: Education, Specialized Skills, Branding).
