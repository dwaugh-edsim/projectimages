Econ 12: Market Spectrum Recap

1. Perfect Competition (The Competitive Ideal)

A market where no single buyer or seller has any power to influence the price. You are a Price Taker.

The 4 Pillars:
  • Many Buyers & Sellers: Thousands of traders; no one is big enough to matter.
  • Identical Products: The goods are exactly the same (e.g., wheat, oil, gold).
  • Perfect Information: Everyone knows the price and quality instantly.
  • Easy Entry & Exit: No barriers to starting or stopping the business.

2. Price Maker vs. Price Taker

  • Price Maker: A firm with enough power (Monopoly/Oligopoly) to set their own price. (e.g., Apple, NS Power).
  • Price Taker: A firm that must accept the market price or sell nothing. (e.g., A potato farmer).

3. The "Race to the Bottom"

In a perfectly competitive market, the only way to survive is to have the lowest cost of production. If someone else can make it cheaper, you go out of business.

The Global Bridge:

Thanks to Containerization (the "Steel Box"), shipping costs are now negligible. This means the whole world is now one giant Pit Market. If labor is cheaper in Bangladesh than in Nova Scotia, the market will naturally move there.

4. Vocabulary to Know

  • Commodity: A raw material or primary agricultural product that can be bought and sold.
  • Invisible Hand: Adam Smith's concept that self-interest leads to market efficiency.
  • Allocative Efficiency: Producing exactly what consumers want at the lowest possible price.
