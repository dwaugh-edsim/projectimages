The Invisible Hand

How "Greed" Creates Order.

In Round 1, you only cared about YOUR profit. You didn't care about the "Market."
But in Round 2, your individual greed combined to create a Stable Equilibrium.
Adam Smith (1776): By following our own self-interest, we benefit society more than we ever intended.

Perfect Competition

The Ideal Market.

Many Traders: No one person can control the price.
Homogenous Product: Every card was the same "good."
Perfect Information: Once the prices were on the board, the chaos stopped.
Result: Efficiency. The price hits the lowest point possible.

The "Marginal" Trader

Who gets left behind?

Did you have a high-cost card when the market price was low? You were "Priced Out."
Competition forces efficiency. It rewards the sellers who can produce for the lowest cost.
In the real world, this signals that you should move your resources elsewhere.

Economic Shocks

Taxes vs. Subsidies.

The $5 Subsidy: Gave Buyers "Superpowers." Prices went UP as they competed harder.
The $5 Teacher Tax: Hurt Sellers. To survive, they raised prices. The price went UP again.
Lesson: Intervention always changes the "Natural" Equilibrium.
