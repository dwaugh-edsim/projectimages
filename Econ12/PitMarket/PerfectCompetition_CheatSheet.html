Perfect Competition

The theoretical ideal of market efficiency.

1. Many Buyers & Sellers

No single person has enough power to change the price. Everyone is a "Price Taker."

2. Identical Products

Consumers don't care who they buy from because the goods are exactly the same (e.g., wheat, salt, gold).

3. Perfect Information

Everyone knows the prices and quality of all goods in the market. No secrets!

4. Easy Entry & Exit

New firms can join the market whenever they want, and struggling firms can leave without cost.

The Spectrum: Perfect vs. Imperfect
