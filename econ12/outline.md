# 📊 Economics 12: Project Presentation Outline

> **Objective:** Deliver a concise, 5-minute briefing on your chosen economic issue. Your presentation will provide the content for the upcoming class test, so clarity and depth are essential.

---

## 🕒 The 5-Minute Breakdown
*   **0:00 - 2:00:** The Context (The 5 W's)
*   **2:00 - 4:30:** The Economic Analysis (Core Issues)
*   **4:30 - 5:00:** The "Big Takeaway" & Final Summary

---

## 📝 1. The Context: Defining the Issue (The 5 W's)
Establish the "ground truth" of your topic. Answer the following questions (Note: Use the "Ws" that fit your specific topic—not every issue will require all five):

*   **WHO:** Which specific groups, industries, or demographics are most affected? (e.g., small business owners in Halifax, Gen Z renters, Canadian auto workers).
*   **WHAT:** What exactly is happening? Define the phenomenon in simple terms.
*   **WHERE:** Is this a local (Nova Scotia), national (Canada), or global issue?
*   **WHEN:** Is this a historical trend reaching a breaking point in 2026, or a sudden economic shock?
*   **WHY:** What are the underlying causes? Why should we care about this right now?

---

## 📈 2. The Economic Analysis
This is the core of your presentation. You must translate the news/issue into **economic theory**.

*   **Identify the Core Concepts:** Use terms from Units 4 & 5 (e.g., Trade Barriers, Tariffs, Supply Chain Disruptions, Income Inequality, Automation, Inflation, or Externalities).
*   **The "Major Takeaway":** If your classmates only remember one thing for the test, what should it be? 
    *   *Example:* "While tariffs protect local jobs, they act as a hidden tax that raises prices for every consumer."
    *   *Example:* "The housing crisis isn't just about supply; it's a massive shift in how wealth is inherited vs. earned."

---

## 🎓 Test Preparation Requirement
**Important:** The next test will be built directly from YOUR presentations. 

*   **Accountability:** If your briefing is unclear, your classmates' grades will suffer (or if they don't pay attention...). 
*   **Clarity:** Ensure all key terms are defined and visible, and ensure you "get" the main idea of your classmates.

---

## 🚀 Pro-Tips for a 5-Minute Pitch
1.  **Don't Read Slides:** Use images and bullet points; tell the story yourself.
2.  **Use Real Numbers:** Instead of saying "it's expensive," say "rent has surged to $2,800/month in Halifax."
3.  **Practice the Timing:** 5 minutes goes by faster than you think. Focus on the *Why* and the *Economic Impact*.
