## Welcome to the Stock Market Simulation (Q4 2026)

### Your Objective
Analyze 7 major corporations across different sectors. Each has a unique risk profile and market position. Choose ONE stock to lock in for the 10-week holiday quarter.

## 1. Artisia Boutique (ARTS)
### Sector: Consumer Discretionary / High-End Fashion
**Risk Profile: HIGH VOLATILITY (Growth)**

*   **About:** Formerly a boutique Vancouver label, Artisia has ascended to global dominance through social media excellence and its tech-integrated "Super Puff" line.
*   **The Edge:** Extreme brand loyalty and viral potential.
*   **The Risk:** Highly sensitive to consumer trends and PR scandals.
*   **HQ:** Vancouver, BC.
*   **Snapshot:** P/E: 42.1 | Market Cap: $8.4B.

---

## 2. Cenovus Energy (CVE)
### Sector: Energy / Natural Resources
**Risk Profile: MEDIUM VOLATILITY (Commodity)**

*   **About:** A massive integrated oil company with strong refining and production assets in the Canadian oil sands.
*   **The Edge:** A natural hedge against geopolitical conflict; oil prices often spike during global instability.
*   **The Risk:** Vulnerable to environmental regulations and global supply shifts.
*   **HQ:** Calgary, AB.
*   **Snapshot:** P/E: 12.5 | Market Cap: $45.2B | Dividend: 2.1%.

---

## 3. Royal Bank of Canada (RBC)
### Sector: Financial Services
**Risk Profile: LOW VOLATILITY (Stability)**

*   **About:** Canada's largest bank and the cornerstone of the national financial system.
*   **The Edge:** Reliable dividends and slow, steady growth. A "Flight to Safety" stock when the market panics.
*   **The Risk:** Moves with interest rate policy; sensitive to slowing domestic housing markets.
*   **HQ:** Toronto, ON.
*   **Snapshot:** P/E: 10.8 | Market Cap: $184B | Dividend: 4.2%.

---

## 4. Lululemon Athletics (LULU)
### Sector: Consumer Discretionary / Wellness
**Risk Profile: MEDIUM VOLATILITY (Growth)**

*   **About:** The global leader in athletic apparel and technical "wellness" wear.
*   **The Edge:** Strong multi-generational appeal and a massive "New Year" sales surge.
*   **The Risk:** High price point makes it sensitive to economic downturns (consumers cutting back).
*   **HQ:** Vancouver, BC.

---

## 5. Netflix Inc. (NFLX)
### Sector: Technology / Entertainment
**Risk Profile: HIGH VOLATILITY (Tech)**

*   **About:** The pioneer of global streaming entertainment with over 250M subscribers.
*   **The Edge:** Thrives during stay-at-home events and winter months. Rapid moves based on blockbuster content.
*   **The Risk:** Intense competition (Streaming Wars) and high cost of original content production.

---

## 6. McDonald's Corp. (MCD)
### Sector: Consumer Staples / Fast Food
**Risk Profile: LOW VOLATILITY (Defensive)**

*   **About:** The world's most recognized restaurant chain and a defensive market play.
*   **The Edge:** People eat fast food in good times and bad. Massive real estate holdings provide deep stability.
*   **The Risk:** Vulnerable to labor strikes and public relations disasters (like pricing "sauce fees").
*   **HQ:** Chicago, IL.

---

## 7. GameStop Corp. (GME)
### Sector: Speculative Asset
**Risk Profile: EXTREME VOLATILITY (Meme Stock)**

*   **About:** A traditional video game retailer that has become a symbol of retail trader coordination.
*   **The Edge:** Can skyrocket 10-15% in a single day based on social media trends or "Short Squeezes."
*   **The Risk:** Does not follow traditional economic math. It can crash just as fast as it rises.
*   **Strategic Warning:** For the bold or the reckless only.
