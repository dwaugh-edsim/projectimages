The Halifax 1% HST Shift: Wealth vs. Utility

In Oct 2024, Nova Scotia cut the HST from 15% to 14%. The government called it "Essential Relief," but critics called it an "Election Gimmick" that cost the province $260 Million a year in lost revenue. That's money that could fund healthcare and housing.
HST applies a flat rate to purchases, but has a "Regressive" effect because lower earners spend a larger share of their paycheck on necessities.
