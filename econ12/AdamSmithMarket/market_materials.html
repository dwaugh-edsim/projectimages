Market Activity Guide

ECONOMICS 12: THE WORLD OF ADAM SMITH

How to Play

  1. Phase 1: Free Trade (2 Mins)
    • Use your $5 starting capital to secure survival goods.
    • Negotiate prices. There is NO fixed cost.
    • Trade goods for other goods (barter) or for money.
  2. The Drought Shock!
    • Teacher announces "DROUGHT SHOCK!"
    • Wheat and Bread supply is immediately halved.
    • Teacher removes all slips marked with the [DROUGHT LOSS] tag.
  3. Phase 2: Scarcity (1 Min)
    • Markets resume. Prices will likely skyrocket.
    • Can you still meet your mission?

Initial Market Rates

Post these on the board as anchors:

  • 🍎 Apples: $1.00
  • 🍞 Bread: $2.00
  • 🌾 Wheat: $1.00

Teacher Setup

  • Give each student 5 x $1 slips ($125 total).
  • Distribute mission cards (1 per student).
  • Spread goods slips on a central "Market Table."

Debrief Discussion

  1. The Shock: Did everything go up, or just bread/wheat? Did you feel "powerful" with rare supply?
  2. Invisible Hand: Did anyone tell you to raise prices, or did it happen naturally? Any desperation trades?
  3. Survival vs Wealth: Who had the most money but failed their mission? Is money useful if there's no bread?
  4. Real World: Can you think of a real-life "shock" (storm, pandemic) that changed prices overnight?
