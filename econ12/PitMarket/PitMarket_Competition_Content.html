The Invisible Hand

How "Greed" Creates Order.

In Round 1, you only cared about YOUR profit. You didn't care about the "Market."
But in Round 2, your individual greed combined to create a Stable Equilibrium.
Adam Smith (1776): By following our own self-interest, we are led as if by an "invisible hand" to benefit society.

Perfect Competition

The Ideal Market.

Many Buyers & Sellers: No one person can control the price.
Homogenous Product: Every card was the same "good."
Perfect Information: Once the prices were on the board, the "cheating" and "overpaying" stopped.
Result: The price hits the lowest possible point that still keeps sellers in business.

The "Marginal" Trader

Who gets left behind?

Did you have a Seller card for $40 when the market price was $25? You were "Priced Out."
Competition forces efficiency. It rewards the sellers who can produce for the lowest cost.
In the real world, this is why some businesses fail while others thrive. It’s not "mean"—it’s a signal to move your resources elsewhere.

Economic Shocks

Taxes vs. Subsidies.

The $5 Subsidy: Gave Buyers "Superpowers." They could afford to pay more, so the price went UP.
The $5 Teacher Tax: Hurt Sellers. To stay profitable, they had to raise their asking price. The price went UP again.
Lesson: Government intervention always changes the "Natural" Equilibrium.
