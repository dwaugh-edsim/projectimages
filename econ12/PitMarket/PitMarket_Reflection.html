Think back to the chaos of the market. In Round 1, prices were all over the place. By Round 2 or 3, they started to settle. Why?

1. In which round was it easier to make a deal? Why do you think that is?

2. Did you ever walk away from a deal? What would have happened if you were FORCED to buy or sell regardless of your card value?

3. Perfect Competition requires "Perfect Information." How did having the previous round's prices on the board change your strategy?

4. If one seller decided to raise their price to $50 when everyone else was selling at $25, what would happen to that seller in a "Perfectly Competitive" market?

5. Economic Efficiency: Did the simulation end with the most "surplus" possible? Who was left out of the market, and why?
