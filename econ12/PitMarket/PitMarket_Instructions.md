# Teacher Guide: The Pit Market Simulation

This simulation demonstrates how a **Perfectly Competitive Market** naturally finds an equilibrium price through the interaction of many buyers and sellers.

## 🕒 Timing: 75 Minutes (Standard Block)
- **Setup:** 5 mins
- **Round 1 (Blind Market):** 8 mins
- **Round 2 (Informed Market):** 8 mins
- **Round 3 (The Teacher Tax):** 12 mins
- **Round 4 (The Subsidy):** 12 mins
- **Final Discussion & Reflection:** 20 mins
- **Admin/Buffer:** 10 mins

## 📦 Materials Needed
- **Buyer Cards:** (Value range $10–$40)
- **Seller Cards:** (Cost range $10–$40)
- **Scoreboard:** (Use the `PitMarket_ClassroomDisplay.html` or the whiteboard)
- **Optional:** Small treats (candy) or "extra credit points" to represent profits.

---

## 🎮 How to Run the Game

### 1. Setup
Divide the class exactly in half: **Buyers** and **Sellers**.
- **Buyers:** Their goal is to buy for **LESS** than the number on their card. Their "Profit" = (Card Value - Price Paid).
- **Sellers:** Their goal is to sell for **MORE** than the number on their card. Their "Profit" = (Price Received - Card Cost).

### 2. Round 1: The "Blind" Market
- Hand out cards randomly.
- **Rule:** No one can see anyone else's card.
- **Process:** Students enter the "Pit" (center of the room) and shout out offers ("Buying for $15!", "Selling for $30!").
- When a deal is made, they come to you together. You record the price on the board and take their cards. They sit down.

### 3. Debrief Round 1
- Look at the prices. They will likely be all over the place ($12, $35, $20).
- Ask: "Why were the prices so different?" (Answer: Poor information).

### 4. Round 2: The "Informed" Market
- Hand out the SAME cards again (or similar).
- **Rule:** This time, keep the prices from Round 1 on the board.
- **The Result:** Prices will cluster tightly around the Equilibrium.

### 5. Round 3: The Government Subsidy
Tell the class: "The government wants to encourage trade, so they are giving every buyer $5 for every successful deal."
- **Rule:** Buyers get an extra $5 from the "government" (you) for every deal.
- **Profit Calculation:** (Card Value - Price Paid + $5).
- **Observation:** Watch the equilibrium price shift as buyers have more "purchasing power."

### 6. Round 4: The "Teacher Tax" (The Facilitation Fee)
Tell the class: "I'm doing all this work facilitating, I'm taking a $5 fee for every deal. Deal with it."
- **Rule:** Sellers must pay YOU $5 for every deal made.
- **Profit Calculation:** (Price Received - Card Cost - $5).
- **Observation:** Watch the equilibrium price rise as sellers try to pass the tax on to buyers.

---

## 🎓 The Lesson (The "Aha!" Moment)
After Round 2, ask the class: 
1. **Did the price stabilize?** (Usually yes).
2. **What happened to the people with high seller costs or low buyer values?** (They couldn't make a deal—they were "priced out").
3. **Is this fair?** (Great debate topic).

---

## 📝 Tips for Success
- **Shouting is encouraged:** The "Pit" should be loud. 
- **No force:** Buyers don't *have* to buy and Sellers don't *have* to sell if they can't make a profit.
- **The "Cheating" Check:** Make sure Sellers don't sell below cost and Buyers don't buy above value!
