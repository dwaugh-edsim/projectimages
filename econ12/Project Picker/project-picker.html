[ PROJECT OPERATIONAL OUTLINE ]

01. THE CONTEXT (5 W's)

Note: Use the "Ws" that fit your topic—not all will apply.

  • WHO: Identify specific groups or industries affected.
  • WHAT: Define the phenomenon in simple terms.
  • WHERE: Specify Local, National, or Global scope.
  • WHEN: Historical trend vs. 2026 shock.
  • WHY: Underlying causes and current relevance.

02. ECONOMIC ANALYSIS

  • CORE CONCEPTS: Connect to Trade, Automation, Inequality, or Inflation.
  • THE TAKEAWAY: Distill your research into one "Big Idea" for the class.
  • DATA: Use real numbers (e.g., "$2,800/mo rent") to anchor your argument.
