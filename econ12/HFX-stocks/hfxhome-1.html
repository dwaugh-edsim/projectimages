The 10-Week Stock Market Challenge

Economics 12: Understanding Risk, Volatility, and Financial Markets

Your Mission

You have been allocated a fictional $10,000 portfolio to invest in a single company for a period of 10 market weeks. This simulation is designed to be a high-stakes, hands-on experience in market decision-making.

Each week, a major Headline will be released, creating volatility and driving your stock's value up or down. Your goal is simple: analyze the initial risk profile of each company and select the investment you believe will yield the highest return over the 10-week period.

Phase 1: Research & Selection

Before Week 1 begins, you must commit to your choice.

  • Review the Investment Prospectus to analyze the seven companies.
  • Choose ONE stock (and only one) to invest your entire $10,000.
  • Record your choice and your initial rationale.

Phase 2: Track & Reflect

For 10 weeks, you will track your portfolio's performance.

  • Use the Portfolio Tracker (Calculator) to log the weekly percentage changes.
  • Calculate your final portfolio value and total return.
  • Be prepared to justify your decisions and reflect on the market outcomes.
