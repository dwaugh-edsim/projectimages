Case File: Swipe-19
The Balance Trap

Today we're taking the role of a Financial Credit Advisor.

You have been assigned to evaluate the file of Michelle Rivera, a 19-year-old student-worker who has run up a credit card balance of $1,162.43 on a $1,500 limit.

Her hours at work have dropped, interest is starting to build, and teaser offers are starting to arrive in the mail. Your mission is to advise her before a small credit squeeze becomes a long-term trap.

Client Message // Michelle Rivera

"I need help making a smart plan before things get worse."

Michelle isn't bankrupt or in collections yet, but she is starting to lean on her credit card to manage cash flow. She needs your advice on several critical questions:

  • Which statement numbers should she prioritize: APR, Available Credit, or Minimum Payments?
  • What mathematical strategy can she use to escape the minimum payment trap?
  • Should she accept a pre-approved travel card to transfer the balance?
  • How will utilization and late payments affect her score and future apartment rentals?
