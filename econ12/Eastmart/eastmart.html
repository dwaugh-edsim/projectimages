Survive the Margins

Economics 12 Outcomes
Students will analyze how non-price determinants (shifts in supply and demand) and external economic indicators (inflation, recession, government policy) impact business viability.

Students will explain how firms in imperfectly competitive markets (Oligopolies/Monopolistic Competition) use product differentiation and marketing, rather than just price, to increase market share.

Mission Briefing

  • Objective: Survive 10 Quarters. Avoid Bankruptcy.
  • Starting Capital: You begin with $5,000.
  • Staff Morale: You now control wages. Low wages = High Profit but Low Productivity. Ignoring morale causes Strikes.
  • Rival AI: Your competitor will poach staff and start price wars. Watch for RIVAL ALERTS.
  • Leakage: Desperate customers steal. High prices = High Theft.
Learning Goals
Analyze impact of external economic indicators (inflation, recession).
Utilize non-price strategies (marketing, differentiation) in an oligopoly market.
